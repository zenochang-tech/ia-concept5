Affiliate Campaigns

TL;DR

Affiliate campaigns are the promotional efforts a publisher builds around a specific advertiser offer, such as a blog post, social push, or email send. Each campaign is tracked separately so results can be measured and optimised.

What are affiliate campaigns?

Affiliate campaigns are the organised promotions a publisher runs to drive traffic toward a specific affiliate offer. A campaign might be a blog article, a social media series, an email send, or a paid ad, each built around one or more affiliate links.

Why publishers structure campaigns

  • Measurement — tagging traffic by campaign shows which content or channel actually drives conversions.
  • Testing — publishers can compare creatives, offers, or timing to see what performs best.
  • Reporting — campaign-level data feeds into the affiliate dashboard, making it easier to justify time and spend.

On Involve Asia

Publishers can generate distinct tracking links for each campaign under an affiliate program, letting them see exactly how each piece of content or channel contributes to their overall results.
