## What is cost per lead (CPL)?

Cost per lead (CPL) is a [commission model](commission-model) where a [publisher](publisher) earns a fixed fee each time they generate a qualifying lead for an [advertiser](advertiser), such as a completed enquiry or application form.

## What qualifies as a lead

- **Form completions** — enquiry forms, quote requests, or applications.
- **Contact details** — a genuine email address or phone number submitted, not a duplicate or test entry.
- **Advertiser-defined criteria** — each offer sets its own definition of a valid lead.

## How it compares

CPL is closely related to [cost per action (CPA)](cost-per-action-cpa) but specifically tied to lead generation, and is often used alongside a [lead magnet](lead-magnet) to encourage form completions, unlike [cost per sale (CPS)](cost-per-sale-cps), which pays only on a completed purchase.
