## What is a direct order?

A direct order is a sale or conversion credited straight to the [publisher](publisher) whose link the customer clicked, with no other publisher's link appearing anywhere in that journey.

## Direct order vs indirect order

- **Direct order** — one publisher, one click, one resulting sale; the clearest case for attribution.
- **Indirect order** — the customer interacted with more than one publisher's link before buying, so [last-click attribution](last-click-attribution) or another rule decides who gets credit; see [indirect order](indirect-order).

## Why it matters

Direct orders are the simplest form of tracked [conversion flow](conversion-flow) and usually the easiest for advertisers to validate, since there's no overlap to resolve before [commission](commission) is confirmed.
