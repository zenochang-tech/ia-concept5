## What is a cookie period?

A cookie period is the length of time, set by the [advertiser](advertiser), during which a purchase made after a [publisher](publisher)'s [click](clicks) still counts as a valid conversion. It is also known as an [attribution window](attribution-window).

## How it works

- **Set per offer** — common periods range from a single day to 30 days or more.
- **Starts at the click** — the countdown begins the moment the customer clicks the tracked link.
- **Uses a tracking cookie** — a [tracking cookie](tracking-cookie), or [cross-device tracking](cross-device-tracking), stores the click so it can be matched later.

## Why it matters

A longer cookie period benefits publishers whose audience takes time to decide, since a sale days after the click can still earn [commission](commission).
