## What is a tracking link?

A tracking link is the unique URL a [publisher](publisher) generates to promote a specific offer. It looks like an ordinary web link but carries identifiers, such as the publisher's ID and offer ID, so that any click, and any sale that follows, is attributed to the right publisher.

## What a tracking link does

- **Redirects the customer** — the link forwards visitors to the advertiser's site, usually to a specific product or landing page.
- **Records the click** — a [click ID](click-id) and timestamp are logged the moment someone clicks.
- **Carries extra tags** — publishers can add a [Sub-ID](sub-id-sub-tracking) to label the traffic source without creating a separate link.

## Tracking link vs deeplink

A standard tracking link usually points to an offer's default landing page, while a [deeplink](deeplink) points to a specific page within the advertiser's site, such as one product or category, while keeping the same tracking. Both are also sometimes called an [affiliate link](affiliate-link), and publishers typically generate them directly from the offer page before sharing them anywhere.
