## What is earnings per conversion?

Earnings per conversion is the average [commission](commission) a [publisher](publisher) earns for each validated conversion, found by dividing total commission by the number of conversions in a given period.

## How it's used

- **Comparing offers** — an offer with a high per-conversion value may be worth prioritising even if it converts less often.
- **Spotting anomalies** — a sudden drop can flag a change in commission rate or an issue with how sales are being validated.

## Earnings per conversion vs earnings per click

[Earnings per click (EPC)](earnings-per-click-epc) looks at value per click sent, while earnings per conversion isolates the value of each completed sale, useful once a publisher already knows their [conversion rate (CVR)](conversion-rate-cvr) is healthy.
