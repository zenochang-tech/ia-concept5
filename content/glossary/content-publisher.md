## What is a content publisher?

A content publisher is a [publisher](publisher) that operates a website or media property built around editorial content, such as articles, guides, or reviews, with [affiliate links](affiliate-link) placed throughout.

## How they earn

- **In-content links** — recommending products naturally within articles.
- **Resource pages** — dedicated "best of" or buying-guide pages built for search traffic.
- **Ongoing publishing** — a steady output of new content to keep earning from search and returning readers.

## Relation to other terms

Content publishers are closely related to [bloggers](blogger) and [content creators](content-creator); the distinction is usually one of scale, with a content publisher typically running a full site rather than a single channel.
