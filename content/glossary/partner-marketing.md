## What is partner marketing?

Partner marketing is an umbrella term for arrangements where businesses work together to reach each other's audiences, rather than marketing alone. It covers a range of setups, from formal [affiliate programs](affiliate-program) to looser referral deals.

## Where affiliate marketing fits in

- **Affiliate programs** — a structured, commission-based version of partner marketing, tracked through links and paid on validated actions.
- **[Referral marketing](referral-marketing)** — a partner-driven variant focused on customers or users recommending a brand directly.
- **Co-marketing and integrations** — broader partnerships, such as joint content or bundled products, that may not involve commission at all.

## Why the distinction matters

Involve Asia specifically runs the affiliate side of partner marketing: tracked, [performance marketing](performance-marketing) where publishers earn based on measurable results, rather than a flat sponsorship fee.
