## What is the minimum withdrawal amount?

The minimum withdrawal amount is the smallest balance a publisher must accumulate in [current earnings](current-earnings) before they can request a payout. Until that threshold is reached, earnings simply carry over to the next period.

## Why it exists

- **Processing cost** — small, frequent transfers cost more, in fees and effort, than they're worth for either side.
- **Currency handling** — thresholds are usually set in the account's [payout currency](payout-currency), so publishers know exactly what they need to reach.

## On Involve Asia

Publishers can track progress toward the threshold from their dashboard, alongside [estimated earnings](estimated-earnings) still pending validation. Once the balance clears the minimum, a withdrawal can be requested, including through [Express Withdrawal](express-withdrawal) where available.
