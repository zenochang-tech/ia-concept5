## What is the deeplink generator?

The deeplink generator is an Involve Asia tool that converts a plain merchant URL into a working [deeplink](deeplink), a tracked link that credits the [publisher](publisher) while sending the visitor to the exact page they entered.

## How it works

- **Paste the URL** — the publisher enters the specific product or category page they want to promote.
- **Check it's deeplinkable** — the tool confirms the offer supports [deeplinking](deeplinkable) before generating a link.
- **Get the tracked link** — the generator returns a [tracking link](tracking-link) that can be shared directly or shortened.

## Why it matters

Building deeplinks manually means correctly appending tracking parameters to a merchant URL, which is easy to get wrong. The generator removes that risk and keeps a record in [deeplink history](deeplink-history) for reuse.
