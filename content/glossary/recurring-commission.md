## What is a recurring commission?

A recurring commission is a [commission](commission) that continues to be paid to a [publisher](publisher) for as long as a customer they referred keeps generating qualifying transactions, such as a monthly subscription renewal. It rewards the publisher for the ongoing value of the customer, not just the first sale.

## How it differs from other models

- **Recurring commission** — paid repeatedly, tied to an ongoing relationship such as a subscription.
- **[One-time commission](one-time-commission)** — paid once, for a single qualifying action.
- **[Lifetime commission](lifetime-commission)** — paid on all future purchases by that customer, not only recurring ones.

## Why it matters

Recurring commission structures suit advertisers with subscription or membership products, since they align the publisher's incentive with retaining customers over time rather than only acquiring them. Publishers should check an offer's [commission model](commission-model) to see whether payouts are recurring, one-time, or lifetime.
