## What is an interstitial?

An interstitial is a full-page ad or promotional screen that appears between one piece of content and the next, for example while a page loads or between app screens, before the user reaches their intended destination.

## Where it's used

- **Between pages** — shown after a user clicks through from search or another page, before the destination content appears.
- **In apps** — commonly used at natural breaks, such as between levels in a game or after completing an action.
- **As a publisher format** — some publishers use interstitials to display an advertiser's offer, counted as an [impression](impression) each time it is shown.

## Why it matters

Because interstitials are hard to ignore, they tend to generate a strong [click-through rate](click-through-rate-ctr), but they can also frustrate users if overused. Publishers using this format should link through to a relevant [landing page](landing-page) so the interruption still feels worthwhile.
