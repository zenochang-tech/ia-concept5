## What is a landing page?

A landing page is the page a user is sent to after clicking a publisher's tracking link, often a specific [offer page](offer-page) or product listing rather than the advertiser's homepage.

## What makes a good landing page

- **Relevance** — it should match what the publisher promoted, so a user who clicked expecting a specific product lands on that product, not a generic page.
- **A clear [call to action](call-to-action-cta)** — an obvious next step, such as "buy now" or "sign up".
- **Deeplinking** — advertisers can use a [deeplink](deeplink) to send traffic straight to a specific product or app screen instead of a generic entry point.

## Why it matters

A mismatched landing page is one of the most common reasons a click fails to convert. Publishers who understand the [conversion flow](conversion-flow) from click to purchase can choose landing pages that keep users moving toward the qualifying action.
