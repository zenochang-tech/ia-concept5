## What is the marketplace?

The marketplace is the section of the platform where [publishers](publisher) browse [affiliate offers](affiliate-offer) from many advertisers in one place, and apply to join the [affiliate programs](affiliate-program) that suit their audience.

## What publishers can do in the marketplace

- **Browse offers** — filter by category, region, or commission model to find relevant programmes.
- **Compare terms** — check each offer's [offer page](offer-page) for the commission rate, cookie period, and other terms before applying.
- **Apply to join** — submit an application, which is approved either through [Instant Apply](instant-apply) or [manual approval](manual-approval), depending on the offer.

## Why it matters

The marketplace is usually a publisher's starting point for finding new offers to promote, making it easier to discover programmes outside the ones they already know, and to compare options before committing traffic to a new advertiser.
