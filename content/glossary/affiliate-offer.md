## What is an affiliate offer?

An affiliate offer is a specific promotion an [advertiser](advertiser) sets up within their [affiliate program](affiliate-program). Each offer defines what a [publisher](publisher) is promoting and what they earn for driving a result.

## What an offer defines

- **Commission** — the rate or fixed amount paid per validated action.
- **Cookie period** — how long a click stays valid for attribution.
- **Traffic rules** — which [allowed traffic sources](allowed-traffic-sources) and [promotion methods](promotion-method) are permitted.
- **Region and product scope** — which countries or products the offer actually covers.

## Why it matters

Publishers browse and apply to offers that match their audience, and each offer's terms tell them exactly how they will be paid and what they can and cannot do to promote it. Advertisers can run multiple offers at once, each tuned to a different publisher type or campaign goal.
