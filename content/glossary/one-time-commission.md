## What is a one-time commission?

A one-time commission is a single payout a publisher earns for a qualifying action, most often a customer's first purchase or sign-up. Once it's paid, that customer's future activity doesn't generate further commission for the same referral.

## One-time vs recurring

- **One-time commission** — paid once per new customer or transaction; the standard model for most retail and e-commerce offers.
- **[Recurring commission](recurring-commission)** — paid repeatedly, for example every month a referred subscriber stays active.
- **[Lifetime commission](lifetime-commission)** — paid on every future purchase a referred customer makes, not just the first.

## Why it matters

Understanding which model an offer uses affects how a publisher forecasts earnings, since a one-time [commission structure](commission-structure) rewards acquiring new customers rather than repeat purchases.
