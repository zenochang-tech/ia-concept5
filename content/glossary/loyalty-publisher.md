## What is a loyalty publisher?

A loyalty publisher is a [publisher](publisher) that operates its own membership or rewards programme, such as points, air miles, or cashback, and shares part of the [commission](commission) it earns with members who shop through its links.

## How loyalty publishers work

- **Membership rewards** — users sign up to a loyalty programme and earn points or cashback on qualifying purchases made through the publisher's links.
- **Aggregated offers** — loyalty publishers typically list many advertisers at once, so members can earn rewards across a wide range of purchases in one place.
- **Overlap with cashback** — a [cashback publisher](cashback-publisher) is one specific type of loyalty publisher, focused on cash rewards rather than points or miles.

## Why it matters

Loyalty publishers tend to drive high purchase intent, since their members are actively looking for a way to earn rewards on a purchase they already plan to make, similar to a [coupon/deals publisher](coupon-deals-publisher) working from the same instinct.
