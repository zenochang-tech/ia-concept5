## What is a product catalog?

A product catalog is the structured list of items an [advertiser](advertiser) makes available for [publishers](publisher) to promote, usually as a file or feed containing each product's name, price, image, category, and destination link. It gives publishers the raw material to build content, listings, or comparisons without manually copying details from the advertiser's site.

## What it is used for

- **Content and comparison sites** — pulling live prices and images into articles or listings.
- **Deeplinking at scale** — generating trackable links for many products at once instead of one by one.
- **Coupon and cashback sites** — matching offers to specific products or categories.

## On Involve Asia

Advertisers who supply a product catalog typically expose it through a [data feed](data-feed), which publishers can browse or download and combine with the [deeplink generator](deeplink-generator) to create tracked links for individual [commissionable products](commissionable-product).
