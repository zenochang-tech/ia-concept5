## What is a commission tier?

A commission tier is a step within a [tiered commission structure](tiered-commission-structure) where a [publisher](publisher)'s [commission rate](commission-rate) rises after they cross a defined threshold, such as a number of sales in a month.

## How tiers work

- **Threshold** — a target, for example 50 validated sales in a calendar month.
- **Higher rate** — once met, the rate for further sales (or sometimes all sales) increases.
- **Reset period** — thresholds typically reset each month or campaign cycle.

## Why advertisers use them

Tiers reward publishers who scale their traffic, encouraging continued investment in an offer rather than a flat, unchanging rate regardless of volume. They can also work alongside a one-off [bonus commission](bonus-commission) for hitting a specific milestone.
