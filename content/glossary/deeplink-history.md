## What is deeplink history?

Deeplink history is the saved list of [deeplinks](deeplink) a [publisher](publisher) has previously created using the [deeplink generator](deeplink-generator), kept so they don't have to rebuild the same link twice.

## What it's useful for

- **Reusing links** — find a link made for a specific product weeks ago instead of generating it again.
- **Checking what's already promoted** — see at a glance which pages or offers already have a working [tracking link](tracking-link).
- **Auditing activity** — useful when reviewing which merchant pages a publisher has actively promoted.

## Why it matters

As a publisher's promotions grow across many products and merchants, deeplink history keeps their tracked links organised and searchable, rather than scattered across spreadsheets or messages.
