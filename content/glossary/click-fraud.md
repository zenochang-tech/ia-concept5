## What is click fraud?

Click fraud is the deliberate generation of fake or artificial clicks on a [tracking link](tracking-link), rather than clicks from genuine, interested users. The goal is usually to inflate performance numbers or, in more advanced cases, to trigger commission without a real customer involved.

## Common forms

- **Bots and scripts** — automated software repeatedly clicks a link to inflate click counts.
- **Click farms** — groups of people are paid to click links with no genuine buying intent.
- **Incentivised clicking** — users are paid or rewarded just to click, unrelated to any real interest in the offer.

## Why it matters

Click fraud distorts metrics like [click-through rate (CTR)](click-through-rate-ctr) and can be a precursor to more serious [affiliate fraud](affiliate-fraud), such as [cookie stuffing](cookie-stuffing) or [click-jacking](click-jacking). Advertisers and platforms monitor for unusual click patterns and can suspend accounts found engaging in it.
