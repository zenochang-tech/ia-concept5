## What is earnings per click (EPC)?

Earnings per click (EPC) is the average [commission](commission) a [publisher](publisher) earns for every [click](clicks) sent to an offer, calculated as total commission divided by total clicks.

## Why publishers use it

EPC gives a single, comparable number across offers with different commission rates and prices.

- **High EPC** — an offer converts well, pays well, or both.
- **Low EPC** — worth checking whether it's the [conversion rate (CVR)](conversion-rate-cvr) or the payout that's weak.

## Why it matters

Because EPC blends both conversion performance and payout size into one figure, publishers often use it alongside [click-through rate (CTR)](click-through-rate-ctr) to decide which offers are worth prioritising in their content or placements.
