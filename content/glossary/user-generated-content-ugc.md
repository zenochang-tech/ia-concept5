## What is user-generated content (UGC)?

User-generated content (UGC) is content, such as a product review, unboxing photo, or short video, created by an everyday customer rather than a brand or professional [content creator](content-creator). Because it comes from real users rather than paid promotion, it's often seen as more trustworthy.

## How it's used in affiliate marketing

- **Social proof** — advertisers feature genuine customer photos or reviews on product and landing pages.
- **Publisher content** — a publisher may round up or reference UGC (such as customer reviews) when writing about a product.
- **Community building** — brands encourage customers to post UGC using a branded hashtag, which can then be reshared.

## UGC vs sponsored content

UGC is created voluntarily by customers and is not paid for, which sets it apart from [sponsored content](sponsored-content) made by an [influencer](influencer) under a paid arrangement. Advertisers value both: UGC for authenticity, and sponsored content for reach and control over messaging, and the two are often used together across [social media](social-media).
