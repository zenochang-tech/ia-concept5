## What is an attribution window?

An attribution window is the length of time after a [publisher](publisher)'s link is clicked during which a resulting sale or lead still counts as a valid conversion. Click outside that window, and the same purchase would not be credited to the publisher.

## How it works

- **Start point** — the window begins the moment a user clicks the tracking link.
- **Duration** — commonly ranges from a few hours to 30 days or more, set by the advertiser per offer.
- **Expiry** — a purchase made after the window closes is not attributed, even if the same user clicked earlier.

## Attribution window vs cookie period

"Attribution window" and "[cookie period](cookie-period)" describe the same concept from slightly different angles: cookie period refers to how long the [tracking cookie](tracking-cookie) itself persists in the browser, while attribution window is the business rule for how long that click stays eligible under the offer's [attribution type](attribution-type).
