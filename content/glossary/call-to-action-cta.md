## What is a call-to-action (CTA)?

A call-to-action (CTA) is the instruction placed near a [tracking link](tracking-link) or button that tells a reader or viewer what to do, such as "shop now", "get the deal", or "sign up today". It is meant to move someone from reading or watching to actually clicking.

## Why it matters for publishers

- **Higher engagement** — a specific, direct CTA usually outperforms a vague one, lifting [click-through rate (CTR)](click-through-rate-ctr).
- **Clearer intent** — a good CTA sets expectations, so the click sent onward is more likely to convert on the [landing page](landing-page).
- **Placement** — CTAs work best close to the point of decision, such as right after a product review or comparison.

## Example

"Read the review" is a weak CTA compared with "Get 20% off with this code", which tells the reader exactly what action to take and what they get for taking it.
