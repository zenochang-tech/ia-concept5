## What is cost per action (CPA)?

Cost per action (CPA) is a [commission model](commission-model) where an [advertiser](advertiser) pays a fixed amount each time a user completes a specific action, rather than a percentage of a sale.

## What counts as an action

- **Sign-ups** — creating an account or subscribing to a newsletter.
- **Quote requests** — submitting details for a service such as insurance or a loan.
- **Free trials** — starting a trial without necessarily making a purchase.

## How it compares

CPA differs from [cost per sale (CPS)](cost-per-sale-cps), which pays on completed purchases, and is similar in structure to [cost per lead (CPL)](cost-per-lead-cpl) or [cost per install (CPI)](cost-per-install-cpi), which apply the same fixed-fee logic to leads and app installs specifically.
