## What is base payout?

Base payout is the default [commission rate](commission-rate) an [advertiser](advertiser) pays a [publisher](publisher) for each validated action on an offer, such as a sale or a lead. It is the rate shown on the offer before any extra incentives are applied.

## Base payout vs bonus payout

- **Base payout** — the standard rate every publisher on the offer earns, for example 4% of the sale value.
- **[Bonus payout](bonus-payout)** — an additional amount layered on top, often tied to volume, a promotional period, or a specific publisher agreement.
- **[Bonus commission](bonus-commission)** — similar to bonus payout, usually used interchangeably to describe the extra earned above the base rate.

## Why it matters

Publishers comparing offers should look at base payout first, since it is the guaranteed rate, and treat bonuses as a possible upside rather than a certainty. Advertisers use base payout to keep programmes predictable while still having room to reward strong performance through an [upsize commission](upsize-commission) or tiered incentive.
