## What is an affiliate account manager?

An affiliate account manager is the dedicated contact who supports a [publisher](publisher) or [advertiser](advertiser) using an [affiliate network](affiliate-network). They guide new partners through [onboarding](onboarding), answer platform questions, and help resolve issues with tracking, payments, or offer terms.

## What they do

- **Onboarding** — setting up new accounts, explaining tools, and helping publishers find suitable offers.
- **Performance support** — reviewing a publisher's results and suggesting ways to improve conversions or earnings.
- **Issue resolution** — investigating missing conversions, payment queries, or tracking discrepancies.
- **Relationship management** — keeping advertisers and publishers aligned on expectations and programme terms.

## Why it matters

A good account manager reduces friction for both sides of the [affiliate program](affiliate-program). Publishers get faster answers and better guidance on what converts, while advertisers get a partner who helps keep their programme running smoothly and their publisher base engaged.
