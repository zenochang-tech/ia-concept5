## What is click-through rate (CTR)?

Click-through rate (CTR) is the number of [clicks](clicks) a link receives divided by the number of times it was shown, or [impressions](impression), expressed as a percentage. It is a measure of how well a placement, headline, or [call-to-action](call-to-action-cta) draws attention.

## How it is calculated

CTR = (clicks ÷ impressions) × 100. For example, a banner shown 1,000 times that receives 20 clicks has a CTR of 2%.

## CTR vs conversion rate

A high CTR does not guarantee good earnings on its own. [Conversion rate (CVR)](conversion-rate-cvr) measures what happens after the click, so publishers generally want strong performance on both: a compelling enough placement to earn the click, and a well-matched offer to convert it into a sale. Comparing CTR across placements helps publishers see which content or positioning is actually driving engagement.
