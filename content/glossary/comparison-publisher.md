## What is a comparison publisher?

A comparison publisher is a type of [publisher](publisher) that builds content or tools comparing products, prices, or offers across multiple [advertisers](advertiser), helping shoppers make a decision before they buy.

## How they operate

- **Comparison tables** — listing prices or features side by side across merchants.
- **Buying guides** — editorial content that recommends options and links out to advertisers.
- **Category focus** — often specialising in one vertical, such as insurance, broadband, or electronics.

## Relation to other publisher types

Comparison publishers overlap closely with [price comparison publishers](price-comparison-publisher) and sometimes with [deal aggregators](deal-aggregator) or [coupon/deals publishers](coupon-deals-publisher), though the emphasis is on structured comparison rather than discount codes alone.
