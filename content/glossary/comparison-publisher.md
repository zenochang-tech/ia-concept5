## What is a comparison publisher?

A comparison publisher is a type of [publisher](publisher) that produces content helping readers choose between two or more genuinely different products, services, or brands competing for the same use case, rather than the same product at different prices. Common formats include "brand A vs brand B" articles, "best X for Y" roundups, and feature-by-feature breakdowns.

## How they differ from price comparison publishers

- **Editorial focus** — a comparison publisher researches and evaluates different options and presents a recommendation.
- **[Price comparison publishers](price-comparison-publisher)** — focus specifically on showing the same product's price across multiple retailers, usually powered by live product feed data.
- **Search intent** — comparison content tends to match high-intent queries from readers who have already decided to buy something and are choosing between specific options.

## On Involve Asia

Comparison publishers operate similarly to [content publishers](content-publisher), producing comparison articles or videos and embedding affiliate links to each brand or product discussed. Since a single piece often covers multiple advertisers, publishers can use the [Deeplink Generator](deeplink-generator) to link directly to each specific product being compared, and should keep content updated as pricing, features, or commission terms change.
