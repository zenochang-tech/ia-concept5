## What is a merchant?

A merchant is the business selling products or services that publishers promote through an affiliate program. It is largely interchangeable with [advertiser](advertiser), though "merchant" is used more often for e-commerce stores with a [product catalogue](product-catalog).

## Merchant vs advertiser

- **Merchant** — commonly used for retail or e-commerce brands with physical or digital goods.
- **Advertiser** — the broader platform term, used for any brand running a program on Involve Asia, whether selling products, services, or generating leads.

## How merchants work with publishers

Merchants set the offer terms: the [commission](commission) rate, the cookie period, and which products qualify. [Publishers](publisher) then drive traffic and sales, and the merchant validates each order before commission is paid out.
