## What are prohibited promotion methods?

Prohibited promotion methods are the [promotion methods](promotion-method) an [advertiser](advertiser) does not allow for a given offer. They are set out in each offer's [terms and conditions](terms-conditions-t-cs), and they exist to protect the advertiser's brand and paid marketing channels from being undercut or misrepresented.

## Common examples

- **Brand bidding** — bidding on the advertiser's own brand name in paid search.
- **Cookie stuffing** — dropping tracking cookies without a genuine click.
- **Misleading claims** — implying an official partnership or discount that does not exist.
- **Spam or unsolicited messaging** — promoting through channels the advertiser has ruled out, such as SMS or forums.

## Why it matters

Promoting through a prohibited method can result in commissions being voided, an offer application being rejected, or in serious cases account action for [compliance](compliance) breaches. Publishers should check an offer's terms before launching a campaign, since prohibited methods vary by advertiser and by market.
