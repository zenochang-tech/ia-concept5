## What is payout currency?

Payout currency is the currency a publisher's earnings are converted into and paid out in, regardless of the [offer currency](offer-currency) used to set the original commission rate.

## Why it can differ from offer currency

- **Multi-market earnings** — a publisher promoting offers across several countries earns in several offer currencies but is usually paid out in one consistent payout currency.
- **Account settings** — payout currency is typically set once on the publisher's account, not per offer.
- **Conversion at withdrawal** — earnings are converted at the point of payout, so the amount received reflects the exchange rate applied at that time.

## On Involve Asia

Payout currency also determines the [minimum withdrawal amount](minimum-withdrawal-amount) threshold a publisher needs to reach, and applies to payouts made through methods like [Express Withdrawal](express-withdrawal).
