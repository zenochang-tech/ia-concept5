## What are affiliate campaigns?

Affiliate campaigns are the organised promotions a [publisher](publisher) runs to drive traffic toward a specific [affiliate offer](affiliate-offer). A campaign might be a blog article, a social media series, an email send, or a paid ad, each built around one or more [affiliate links](affiliate-link).

## Why publishers structure campaigns

- **Measurement** — tagging traffic by campaign shows which content or channel actually drives conversions.
- **Testing** — publishers can compare creatives, offers, or timing to see what performs best.
- **Reporting** — campaign-level data feeds into the [affiliate dashboard](affiliate-dashboard), making it easier to justify time and spend.

## On Involve Asia

Publishers can generate distinct tracking links for each campaign under an [affiliate program](affiliate-program), letting them see exactly how each piece of content or channel contributes to their overall results.
