## What are current earnings?

Current earnings is the running total of commission a [publisher](publisher) has accrued on their [affiliate dashboard](affiliate-dashboard), covering everything tracked so far in the period.

## What it includes

The figure usually blends earnings at different stages:

- **Pending** — conversions tracked but not yet through [validation](validation).
- **Approved** — conversions confirmed by the advertiser and ready to be paid.
- **Adjusted** — earnings can drop if a sale is later cancelled or returned.

## Current earnings vs estimated earnings

Current earnings reflects what has actually been tracked, while [estimated earnings](estimated-earnings) can include projections. Publishers should treat current earnings as the more reliable figure for understanding real [affiliate earnings](affiliate-earnings) at any given point.
