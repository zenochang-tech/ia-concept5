## What is conversion flow?

Conversion flow describes the full path a tracked interaction takes, from the initial [click](clicks) through [conversion creation](conversion-creation) to final [validation](validation), at which point [commission](commission) is confirmed.

## The typical stages

- **Click** — a user clicks a publisher's tracked link.
- **Conversion creation** — the advertiser's system records the resulting sale or lead, at whatever [conversion level](conversion-level) it tracks.
- **Validation** — the advertiser confirms the action is genuine and not cancelled.
- **Payout** — validated commission becomes payable to the publisher.

## Why it matters

Understanding the conversion flow helps publishers see where a sale might be lost, whether at the click stage due to a lapsed [cookie period](cookie-period), or later during validation.
