## What is a Sub-ID?

A Sub-ID (also called sub tracking) is a custom identifier a [publisher](publisher) adds to their [tracking link](tracking-link) to label the source of a click, such as a specific blog post, ad placement, email send, or social channel. It does not change where the link goes, only how that click is tagged for reporting.

## Why publishers use it

- **Performance comparison** — see which page, placement, or campaign converts best.
- **Sub-affiliate management** — a larger publisher can tag traffic from each [sub-affiliate](sub-affiliate) separately.
- **Budget decisions** — identify which traffic sources are worth investing more time or ad spend in.

## On Involve Asia

Sub-IDs appear alongside each [click ID](click-id) in reporting, so a publisher running the same offer across several channels can see, at a glance, which one is generating clicks, conversions, and commission, without needing separate tracking links for every source.
