## What is an offer name?

The offer name is the title an advertiser assigns to an offer, for example "Summer Sale 20% Cashback" or "New Customer Sign-up". It is what publishers see when browsing offers, and what appears throughout reporting.

## Why naming matters

- **Findability** — a clear, descriptive name makes it easier for publishers to find relevant offers among a merchant's [product catalogue](product-catalog).
- **Multiple offers per merchant** — a single advertiser may run several offers at once (different regions, products, or seasons), each needing its own name to avoid confusion.
- **Reporting** — commission and click reports are broken down by offer name, so publishers can see exactly which campaign is performing.

## On Involve Asia

The offer name appears on the [offer page](offer-page) along with its terms, region, and currency, giving publishers the full picture before they promote it.
