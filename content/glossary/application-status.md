## What is application status?

Application status is the current state of a [publisher](publisher)'s request to join an advertiser's [affiliate program](affiliate-program). It tells the publisher, and the advertiser, exactly where things stand before any promotion can begin.

## Typical statuses

- **Pending** — the application has been submitted and is awaiting review.
- **Approved** — the publisher has passed [publisher vetting](publisher-vetting) or [manual approval](manual-approval) and can start promoting the offer.
- **Rejected** — the advertiser has declined the application, often with a reason such as audience mismatch or policy conflict.
- **Suspended** — a previously approved publisher has had access withdrawn, for example after an [account suspension](account-suspension).

## Why it matters

Publishers cannot generate tracking links or earn [commission](commission) for an offer until their application status is approved. Checking status regularly helps publishers know which offers they can actively promote versus which are still under review.
