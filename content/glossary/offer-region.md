## What is offer region?

Offer region is the country or market that an offer is set up to serve. A merchant selling only in Malaysia, for example, will list its offer region as Malaysia, and traffic from elsewhere may not be eligible.

## Why it matters

- **Commission eligibility** — some offers only pay out for sales from a [commissionable country](commissionable-country), regardless of where the click came from.
- **Audience fit** — publishers should match the offer region to their own [audience country](audience-country) to avoid wasted promotion.
- **Currency and pricing** — the region usually determines the [offer currency](offer-currency) and local pricing shown to customers.

## On Involve Asia

Offer region is stated clearly on every offer page. Publishers running traffic across multiple countries should check it for each offer individually, since the same merchant can run separate offers for different regions.
