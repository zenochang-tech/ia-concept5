## What is a promotion method?

A promotion method is the channel or technique a [publisher](publisher) uses to bring an audience to an [advertiser](advertiser)'s [offer](affiliate-offer). Every offer specifies which promotion methods are [allowed](allowed-traffic-sources) and which are not, since different advertisers have different rules about how their brand can be marketed.

## Common promotion methods

- **Content** — blog posts, reviews, and comparison articles.
- **Social media** — organic posts or influencer content on platforms such as Instagram or TikTok.
- **Email marketing** — sending offers to a subscriber list.
- **Search engine marketing (SEM)** — paid search ads.
- **Search engine optimisation (SEO)** — ranking organically for relevant search terms.

## Why it matters

Choosing the right promotion method affects both performance and eligibility. Using a method an advertiser has [prohibited](prohibited-promotion-methods) can void commissions, so publishers should match their promotion method to the offer's stated terms before running a campaign.
