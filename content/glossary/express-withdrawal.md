## What is express withdrawal?

Express withdrawal is an Involve Asia payout option that lets eligible [publishers](publisher) cash out earnings from conversions still in the "pending validation" stage, before the [advertiser](advertiser) has reviewed or confirmed the sale. It is the earliest point in the commission lifecycle at which funds can be released, ahead of both [early withdrawal](early-withdrawal) and a standard withdrawal.

## Eligibility and how it compares

- **Standard withdrawal** — pays out once a conversion is validated and meets the [minimum withdrawal amount](minimum-withdrawal-amount).
- **Early withdrawal** — releases commissions that are already approved, sooner than the advertiser's usual cycle.
- **Express withdrawal** — releases part of a still-unvalidated conversion, so it carries stricter eligibility checks: a minimum pending-validation balance of $50, at least one offer active for more than 5 days, and a review of account standing and conversion quality.

## Why it matters

Because express withdrawal draws on unvalidated earnings, it carries more platform risk, so publishers with high rejection rates, a history of fraud, or offer violations can be blocked from using it. Approved requests are typically paid within 5 to 7 working days, excluding weekends and Malaysian public holidays.
