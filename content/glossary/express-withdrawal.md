## What is Express Withdrawal?

Express Withdrawal is an Involve Asia feature that lets [publishers](publisher) access a portion of their pending earnings before an advertiser finishes [validation](validation) of the underlying sale. Standard withdrawal only pays out once a conversion is validated; Express Withdrawal moves part of that money sooner.

## How it compares to standard withdrawal

- **Standard withdrawal** — available once earnings are validated and meet the [minimum withdrawal amount](minimum-withdrawal-amount), typically paid within 7 to 10 working days of the request.
- **Express Withdrawal** — a portion of pending, not-yet-validated earnings can be released once a request is approved, often within 5 to 7 working days.
- **Eligibility** — open to any publisher who meets the platform's eligibility criteria; each request goes through a quick approval step before funds are released.

## Why it matters

For publishers who need cash flow before a sale finishes validating, Express Withdrawal shortens the gap between driving a result and getting paid for it, without waiting for the standard cycle.
