## What is an affiliate program?

An affiliate program is the structure an [advertiser](advertiser) runs to recruit [publishers](publisher) who promote their products or services in exchange for [commission](commission). It is the container that holds one or more [affiliate offers](affiliate-offer).

## What it typically includes

- **Commission terms** — the rates, models, and payout rules for each offer under the programme.
- **Approval process** — how publishers apply and get vetted before they can promote.
- **Traffic and promotion rules** — which channels and methods are allowed or restricted.
- **Creative assets** — banners, product feeds, or [deeplinks](deeplink) publishers can use.

## Why it matters

An affiliate program lets an advertiser scale marketing through a network of independent publishers rather than a single in-house team, paying only for results. For publishers, it is the entry point: they apply to a programme, get approved, and then start promoting its offers.
