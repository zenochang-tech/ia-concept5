## What is a publisher?

A publisher is the partner on the earning side of affiliate marketing. Using a trackable link, a publisher promotes an [advertiser's](advertiser) products to their audience and earns a [commission](commission) every time someone buys or completes a qualifying action.

"Publisher" is a broad, umbrella term. It covers many models:

- **Creators** — influencers and video makers who share products with their followers.
- **Content sites** — blogs, review sites, and news outlets that add links to their articles.
- **Cashback and rewards sites** — that share part of the commission back with their members.
- **Coupon and comparison sites** — that surface deals people are already searching for.
- **App owners and media buyers** — who show offers in-app or run paid traffic at scale.
