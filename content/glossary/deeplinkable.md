## What does deeplinkable mean?

Deeplinkable describes whether a specific URL, product page, or [offer](offer-page) can be converted into a working [deeplink](deeplink). Not every merchant page supports this, depending on how the advertiser has set up their tracking.

## Why some pages aren't deeplinkable

- **Tracking restrictions** — some advertisers only allow tracking through their homepage or a limited set of landing pages.
- **Technical limits** — certain page structures or app screens aren't set up to carry tracking parameters correctly.

## Why it matters

Before building a link with the [deeplink generator](deeplink-generator), it's worth checking whether the target page is deeplinkable. Promoting a page that isn't can mean the link falls back to the homepage, or that clicks aren't tracked as intended through the [tracking link](tracking-link).
