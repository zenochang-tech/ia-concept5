## What is an affiliate dashboard?

An affiliate dashboard is the web interface a [publisher](publisher) uses to monitor their activity on an [affiliate platform](affiliate-platform). It brings together clicks, conversions, [affiliate earnings](affiliate-earnings), and offer status in one place.

## What it typically shows

- **Performance data** — clicks, conversion rate, and [current earnings](current-earnings) by offer or campaign.
- **Tracking tools** — the ability to generate or manage links for approved offers.
- **Payment history** — past payouts and upcoming withdrawal schedules.
- **Offer catalogue** — available programmes the publisher can apply to or is already approved for.

## Why it matters

The dashboard is where day-to-day decisions get made: which offers are converting, where to focus content, and when earnings cross a withdrawal threshold. A clear, real-time dashboard helps publishers optimise without waiting on manual reports from an [advertiser](advertiser).
