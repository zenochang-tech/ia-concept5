## What are missing conversions?

Missing conversions are sales or leads that a publisher actually generated but that never showed up as a tracked conversion. The click happened and the customer converted, but something broke along the way, so the [conversion creation](conversion-creation) never fired.

## Common causes

- **Broken or blocked tracking** — an ad blocker, a disabled cookie, or a misfiring [pixel](pixel-tracking) or [postback URL](postback-url).
- **App-to-web handoffs** — a user switching between an app and a browser can lose the tracking reference.
- **Attribution window gaps** — a sale that lands just outside the tracked window.

## What publishers can do

Most platforms, including Involve Asia, let publishers submit proof (order confirmation, timestamps, screenshots) so the advertiser can manually review and add a missing conversion. This keeps publishers from losing commission over a tracking fault rather than a lack of performance.
