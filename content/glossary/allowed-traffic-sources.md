## What are allowed traffic sources?

Allowed traffic sources are the channels an [advertiser](advertiser) explicitly permits [publishers](publisher) to use when promoting one of their offers. They set the boundaries of a legitimate campaign before a publisher starts sending traffic.

## Common examples

- **Organic search traffic** — visitors arriving through unpaid search results.
- **Email marketing** — sending offers to a publisher's own subscriber list.
- **Social media** — posts, stories, or influencer content on social platforms.
- **Content and comparison sites** — articles, reviews, or comparison tables that link out to the offer.

## Why it matters

Advertisers use this list to protect their brand and budget, for example avoiding channels that could clash with their own advertising or attract low-quality traffic. Promoting an offer through a source that is not on the allowed list, or that appears under [restricted traffic sources](restricted-traffic-sources), can lead to reversed commission or [account suspension](account-suspension).
