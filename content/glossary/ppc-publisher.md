## What is a PPC publisher?

A PPC (pay-per-click) publisher promotes offers by running paid advertisements on search engines, generating [paid search traffic](paid-search-traffic) rather than relying on organic content or an existing audience.

## Rules PPC publishers commonly face

- **Negative keywords** — advertisers usually publish a [negative keyword](negative-keyword) list that PPC publishers must exclude from their campaigns.
- **Brand bidding restrictions** — bidding on the advertiser's own brand terms is often prohibited, since it counts as [brand bidding](brand-bidding).
- **Prior approval** — because paid search can be aggressive and costly to the advertiser, many offers require PPC publishers to apply for specific approval before running campaigns.

## Why it matters

PPC publishing can drive high volumes quickly, but it carries more compliance risk than other publisher types. Publishers running this model should read an offer's promotion method rules carefully before bidding on any keyword.
