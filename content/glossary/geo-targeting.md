## What is geo-targeting?

Geo-targeting is the practice of tailoring an offer, ad, or campaign to users in a specific country or region, rather than showing it to everyone. On an affiliate platform, it determines where a publisher's traffic can convert and earn commission.

## How it's used

- **Audience country** — advertisers may restrict an offer to a set of allowed [audience countries](audience-country), so only traffic from those markets qualifies.
- **Region lock** — some offers apply a [region lock](region-lock), blocking access or tracking entirely outside approved markets.
- **Commissionable country** — even when traffic is allowed, only sales from a [commissionable country](commissionable-country) actually pay out.

## Why it matters

Geo-targeting keeps advertiser budgets focused on markets they can actually serve or ship to, and it also affects a publisher's [click origin country](click-origin-country): traffic from the wrong location may be tracked but never validated.
