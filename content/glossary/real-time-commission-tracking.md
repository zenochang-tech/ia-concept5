## What is real-time commission tracking?

Real-time commission tracking is a system that records a [commission](commission) as soon as the underlying click or conversion happens, rather than waiting for a scheduled batch update. Publishers see new activity reflected in their dashboard within moments instead of hours or days.

## How it works

- **Click and conversion events** fire immediately through tracking technology such as a pixel or [postback URL](postback-url).
- **The platform matches each event** to the publisher and offer that generated it.
- **Figures update live** in the [affiliate dashboard](affiliate-dashboard), including clicks, conversions, and estimated earnings.

## Why it matters

Real-time tracking gives publishers faster feedback on which content or campaigns are working, so they can adjust promotion methods quickly rather than waiting for delayed reports. It also gives advertisers clearer, more current visibility into campaign performance.
