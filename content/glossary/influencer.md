## What is an influencer?

An influencer is a type of [publisher](publisher) who has built a following on [social media](social-media) and earns [commission](commission) by recommending advertiser products or services to that audience, usually through posts, videos, or stories.

## How influencers promote offers

- **Sponsored content** — [sponsored content](sponsored-content) featuring a product, paired with a tracking link or promo code in the caption or bio.
- **Reviews and demos** — short-form video or stories showing a product in use.
- **Affiliate links in bio** — a single trackable link, often placed in a profile or link-in-bio tool, that leads to multiple offers.

## Influencer vs KOL

On many Southeast Asian platforms, [KOL (Key Opinion Leader)](kol-key-opinion-leader) is used interchangeably with influencer to describe the same type of publisher. Both are commonly grouped with [content creators](content-creator) as a distinct publisher category on Involve Asia.
