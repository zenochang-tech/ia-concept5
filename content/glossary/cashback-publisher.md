## What is a cashback publisher?

A cashback publisher is a [publisher](publisher) that runs a site where shoppers click through to advertiser offers and, after the purchase is validated, receive a portion of the [commission](commission) back as cash. The publisher keeps the remainder as its own earnings.

## How it works

- **Shopper clicks through** — the user visits an offer via the cashback site's [tracking link](tracking-link).
- **Purchase is validated** — the advertiser confirms the sale meets the offer's terms.
- **Cashback is shared** — the publisher splits the commission, paying part to the shopper and keeping the rest.

## On Involve Asia

Cashback publishers are one of several publisher types alongside [loyalty publishers](loyalty-publisher) and [coupon/deals publishers](coupon-deals-publisher). Because they rely on repeat, price-conscious shoppers, they tend to perform well on offers with a clear, competitive rate and a shorter [cookie period](cookie-period).
