## What is an advertiser?

An advertiser, also called a merchant or brand, is the company on the paying side of an affiliate marketing relationship. They have a product or service to sell, a goal they want to achieve, and a budget set aside for performance-based marketing.

Advertisers partner with affiliate platforms like Involve Asia to reach a wider network of [publishers](publisher) who promote their offers in exchange for a [commission](commission). The advertiser only pays when a qualifying action is delivered. No result, no cost.

## The advertiser's role

Advertisers are responsible for every element of the offer that partners promote:

- **Setting the commission structure** — which model applies, how much is paid per action, and whether the payout is a flat fee or a percentage of sale value.
- **Defining the qualifying action** — a confirmed purchase, a completed registration, an app install, or another measurable event.
- **Approving partners** — some offers allow instant approval; others need manual review to check the fit.
- **Providing creative assets** — banners, copy, and product images partners can use.
- **Validating conversions** — confirming a tracked sale is genuine before it pays out.

## Advertiser vs publisher

The advertiser funds the commissions; the [publisher](publisher) earns them by driving traffic and sales. Together they form the two sides of every affiliate partnership.
