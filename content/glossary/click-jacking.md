## What is click-jacking?

Click-jacking is a technique where a page is designed, often using an invisible overlay, so that a user clicking on what looks like one thing actually triggers a hidden [tracking link](tracking-link) instead. The user is usually unaware any affiliate click has happened at all.

## How it differs from other fraud

- **[Click fraud](click-fraud)** — generates clicks that never had a genuine user behind them at all.
- **[Cookie stuffing](cookie-stuffing)** — drops tracking cookies without any click at all.
- **Click-jacking** — hijacks a real click from a real user, but for a purpose they never intended.

## Why it matters

Click-jacking is a form of [affiliate fraud](affiliate-fraud) and is prohibited under every affiliate programme's terms. It unfairly credits a publisher for traffic they did not honestly earn, and accounts found using it are typically suspended and have commission reversed.
