## What is paid search traffic?

Paid search traffic is visitors who arrive at a publisher's page or link through a paid advertisement shown on a search engine, rather than an [organic search](organic-search-traffic) result. Publishers who specialise in this are often called [PPC publishers](ppc-publisher).

## Why advertisers restrict it

- **Brand bidding** — bidding on the advertiser's own brand name can compete with the advertiser's own ads, a practice usually restricted and called [brand bidding](brand-bidding).
- **Keyword conflicts** — advertisers often publish a [negative keyword](negative-keyword) list to keep publisher campaigns from overlapping with their own search strategy.
- **Cost control** — since paid traffic is more aggressive, offers often require prior approval before a publisher can run it.

## On Involve Asia

Whether paid search is allowed, and under what conditions, is listed in each offer's promotion method and traffic source rules. Publishers should confirm this before launching a campaign.
