## What is a region lock?

A region lock is a restriction that limits an [offer](affiliate-offer) to specific countries or regions, so that only clicks, conversions, or customers from those locations are eligible to earn [commission](commission). Traffic from outside the locked region is typically tracked but not paid.

## How it is applied

- **By [offer region](offer-region)** — the offer is only open to publishers or audiences in certain markets.
- **By [commissionable country](commissionable-country)** — only sales shipped to or billed from approved countries qualify.
- **By [audience country](audience-country)** — the publisher's audience location is checked against the offer's rules.

## Why it matters

Advertisers use region locks when a product, pricing, or licence only applies in certain markets. Publishers should check an offer's region lock alongside its [geo-targeting](geo-targeting) settings before promoting it to an international audience, to avoid driving traffic that will not be commissioned.
