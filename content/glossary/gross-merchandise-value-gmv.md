## What is gross merchandise value (GMV)?

Gross merchandise value, or GMV, is the total value of all sales made through an offer or platform over a given period, before [commission](commission), fees, returns, or cancellations are deducted. It reflects the scale of transactions, not the profit anyone makes from them.

## GMV vs commission

- **GMV** — the full order value, for example the $50 total of a customer's cart.
- **Commission** — the [commission rate](commission-rate) applied to that order, for example 4% of $50, is what the publisher actually earns.
- **[Total sales](total-sales)** — often used alongside GMV to describe the number or value of orders in a reporting period.

## Why it matters

Advertisers track GMV to understand the overall sales volume an affiliate channel drives, while publishers usually care more about their own commission. A high GMV does not always mean high earnings, since it depends on the rate applied and each order's [average order value](average-order-value-aov).
