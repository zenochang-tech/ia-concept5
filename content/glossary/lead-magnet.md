## What is a lead magnet?

A lead magnet is something of value, such as a free guide, checklist, discount code, or trial, offered to a user in exchange for their contact details. It is a common way to drive sign-ups on [cost per lead (CPL)](cost-per-lead-cpl) offers.

## How publishers use lead magnets

- **Content offers** — an ebook, template, or comparison guide relevant to the advertiser's product.
- **A clear [call to action](call-to-action-cta)** — directing the user to a sign-up form or [landing page](landing-page) to claim it.
- **Follow-up via [email marketing](email-marketing)** — once captured, the lead can be nurtured further, sometimes generating additional value beyond the initial commission.

## Why it matters

A strong lead magnet increases the number of people willing to hand over their details, which directly increases the volume of leads, and therefore commission, a publisher can generate from a CPL offer.
