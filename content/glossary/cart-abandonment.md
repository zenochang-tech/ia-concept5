## What is cart abandonment?

Cart abandonment happens when a shopper adds one or more items to their online cart but exits before completing the purchase. It is one of the most common breaks in the [conversion flow](conversion-flow), sitting between a click and a finished sale.

## Why it matters

- **Lower conversion rate** — a high abandonment rate directly lowers an offer's [conversion rate (CVR)](conversion-rate-cvr), which affects how attractive it looks to publishers.
- **Lost commission** — a publisher only earns [commission](commission) when the sale is actually completed, so an abandoned cart means no payout even though the click happened.
- **Fixable causes** — unexpected costs, a clunky checkout, or a confusing [landing page](landing-page) are frequent reasons shoppers abandon.

## For publishers

Choosing offers from advertisers with a smooth, trustworthy checkout tends to reduce cart abandonment and improve overall earnings, since more clicks make it through to a validated sale.
