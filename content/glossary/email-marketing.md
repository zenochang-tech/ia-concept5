## What is email marketing?

Email marketing is a [promotion method](promotion-method) where a [publisher](publisher) sends offers, deals, or [affiliate links](affiliate-link) directly to a list of subscribers by email, rather than relying on someone finding a website.

## How it's used

- **Newsletters** — regular emails featuring curated deals or new offers.
- **Dedicated campaigns** — a single email built around one promotion or launch.
- **Automated sequences** — triggered emails, such as a welcome series, that include affiliate links over time.

## Why it matters

Email marketing gives a publisher direct, repeatable access to an audience without depending on search or social platforms. Advertisers sometimes restrict how their brand or offers can be used in email, so publishers running this channel should always check the [promotion method](promotion-method) rules on each offer.
