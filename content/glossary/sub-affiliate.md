## What is a sub-affiliate?

A sub-affiliate is a [publisher](publisher) who promotes offers through another, larger publisher's account rather than applying to the [affiliate program](affiliate-program) directly. The larger publisher (sometimes running its own mini [affiliate network](affiliate-network)) recruits, onboards, and supports a group of sub-affiliates, then shares out [commission](commission) based on the traffic and sales each one drives.

## How it works

- **Recruitment** — the parent publisher brings in individual creators, sites, or communities as sub-affiliates.
- **Shared tracking** — sub-affiliate traffic is usually tagged with a [Sub-ID](sub-id-sub-tracking) so it can be separated out in reporting.
- **Revenue share** — the parent publisher typically keeps a portion of the commission earned by its sub-affiliates in exchange for managing them.

## Why it matters

Sub-affiliate structures let an affiliate program scale reach without vetting every individual promoter directly, since the parent publisher takes on that responsibility. Advertisers still expect the same program rules and compliance standards to apply, regardless of how many layers of sub-affiliates sit underneath the main publisher account.
