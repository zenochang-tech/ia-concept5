## What is manual commission tracking?

Manual commission tracking is a process where conversions are recorded and confirmed by hand, for example by uploading an advertiser's sales report, instead of being captured automatically through a [postback URL](postback-url), pixel, or API.

## When it's used

- **No automated integration** — some advertisers, especially offline or newly onboarded ones, do not yet have automated tracking set up.
- **[Conversion creation](conversion-creation)** — conversions are added to the system manually, based on data the advertiser supplies separately.
- **Reconciliation** — manual tracking is also used to fix [missing conversions](missing-conversions) that automated tracking failed to capture.

## Manual vs real-time tracking

Manual commission tracking is slower and more prone to delay than [real-time commission tracking](real-time-commission-tracking), where conversions are captured and confirmed automatically as they happen. It is generally treated as a fallback rather than the preferred method.
