## What is a commission rate?

The commission rate is the specific figure that determines how much a [publisher](publisher) earns from a [commission](commission). It might be a percentage of the sale value (for example, 5% of each order) or a flat amount per action.

## How rates vary

- **By commission model** — a percentage rate suits cost-per-sale offers, while a flat rate suits cost-per-lead or cost-per-install offers.
- **By commission tier** — some advertisers pay a higher rate once a publisher passes a sales threshold, known as a [commission tier](commission-tier).
- **By product** — rates can differ across categories within the same offer, depending on the [commission structure](commission-structure).

## On Involve Asia

Each offer lists its commission rate clearly, so publishers can compare opportunities and estimate potential earnings before choosing what to promote.
