## What is an impression?

An impression is a single instance of an ad, banner, or tracking link being displayed to a user, regardless of whether they interact with it. It is one of the most basic units of measurement in digital and affiliate marketing.

## Impressions vs clicks

- **Impression** — counted every time the content is shown, even if the user scrolls past it.
- **[Clicks](clicks)** — counted only when a user actually interacts with the link or ad.
- **[Click-through rate (CTR)](click-through-rate-ctr)** — the ratio of clicks to impressions, showing how effective the placement is at generating interest.

## Why it matters

Impressions alone do not earn a publisher commission, since most affiliate models pay on validated actions rather than views. Still, tracking impressions alongside [earnings per click](earnings-per-click-epc) helps publishers understand how much visibility it takes to generate one paying result.
