## What is a deal aggregator?

A deal aggregator is a [publisher](publisher) whose site or app collects discounts, promo codes, and offers from many [merchants](merchant) in one place, rather than focusing on a single brand.

## How it differs from other publisher types

- **Coupon/deals publishers** — often focus on codes and vouchers; a deal aggregator may go further, using feeds to keep listings current at scale.
- **Cashback publishers** — return part of a purchase as cashback; an aggregator's core value is choice and comparison rather than a rebate.

## Why it matters

Because a deal aggregator sends traffic to many merchants at once, it often relies on [data feeds](data-feed) to stay current across dozens of offers, and depends heavily on accurate tracking so each sale is credited to the right advertiser and offer.
