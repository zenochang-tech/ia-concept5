## What is a blogger?

A blogger is a type of [publisher](publisher) who runs their own blog and earns [commission](commission) by writing about products or services and linking to advertiser offers. Their content typically includes reviews, comparisons, tutorials, or curated recommendations.

## How bloggers earn

- **Contextual links** — placing a [tracking link](tracking-link) or [deeplink](deeplink) within an article, so a reader who clicks through and converts earns the blogger a commission.
- **Long-form content** — reviews and how-to guides tend to convert well because readers arrive already interested in the topic.
- **Search traffic** — many blogs rely on [organic search traffic](organic-search-traffic), so evergreen content can keep earning long after it is published.
