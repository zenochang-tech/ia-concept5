## What is an app publisher?

An app publisher is a [publisher](publisher) type that promotes advertiser offers through their own mobile app instead of a website. Common examples include cashback apps, rewards apps, and deal-aggregator apps.

## How they operate

- **In-app offers** — advertiser deals and links are presented directly inside the app's interface.
- **User accounts** — many app publishers track individual users so they can pay out cashback or rewards.
- **Mixed tracking** — some conversions rely on standard click tracking, others on [app install tracking](app-install-tracking) if the advertiser's own offer is app-based.

## Why it matters

App publishers often drive high-intent traffic, since users open the app specifically to find deals before buying. For advertisers, this makes them a valuable channel for CPS and CPI offers alike, provided the app population matches the advertiser's target market.
