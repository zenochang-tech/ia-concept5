## What is an affiliate storefront?

An affiliate storefront is a page or site a [publisher](publisher) builds to showcase products or deals from one or more advertisers, with [affiliate links](affiliate-link) built into every listing. It functions like a shop window, even though the publisher never handles the products or payment themselves.

## How it is used

- **Curation** — the publisher selects products across multiple advertisers, organised by category or theme.
- **Monetisation** — every outbound link is an affiliate link, so browsing that leads to a sale earns commission.
- **Common formats** — deal aggregator sites, cashback storefronts, and creator-run "shop my picks" pages.

## Who runs them

Storefronts are common among [cashback publishers](cashback-publisher), [content publishers](content-publisher), and deal aggregators, since the format suits an audience that is already browsing to buy rather than reading a single review or article.
