## What is onboarding?

Onboarding is the process of getting a new account ready to use on an affiliate platform. For publishers, this covers applying, going through [publisher vetting](publisher-vetting), and applying to individual offers. For advertisers, it covers setting up a program, configuring tracking, and publishing offers.

## What onboarding usually involves

- **Application review** — checking the applicant's details and, for publishers, their traffic sources and content.
- **Account setup** — configuring payout details, currency, and platform access.
- **Guidance** — many platforms assign an [affiliate account manager](affiliate-account-manager) to help new accounts get started correctly.

## Why it matters

Good onboarding reduces early mistakes, such as a publisher promoting through a restricted channel or an advertiser launching an offer with incomplete tracking. Publishers can usually track where they stand through their [application status](application-status) while onboarding is in progress.
