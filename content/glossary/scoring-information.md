## What is scoring information?

Scoring information is the set of performance and quality data collected on a [publisher](publisher), used to assess how well they are likely to perform, or have performed, for an [advertiser](advertiser). It can include past conversion rates, traffic quality, compliance history, and account activity.

## What it is used for

- **[Publisher vetting](publisher-vetting)** — helping an advertiser decide whether to approve a new applicant.
- **[Application status](application-status)** decisions — supporting manual or automated approval choices.
- **[Commission tier](commission-tier)** placement — some programmes use scoring to place publishers into higher or lower reward tiers.

## Why it matters

Scoring information gives advertisers an objective basis for decisions that would otherwise rely on guesswork, and it gives strong-performing publishers a route to better terms as their track record builds up.
