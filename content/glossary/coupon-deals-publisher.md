## What is a coupon/deals publisher?

A coupon/deals publisher is a type of [publisher](publisher) whose site or app is built around listing [promo codes](promo-code), vouchers, and limited-time discounts from multiple merchants.

## How it works

Visitors search or browse for a discount before checking out elsewhere, then click through to the merchant using the publisher's tracked link. If they complete a qualifying purchase, the publisher earns a commission.

- **Code publishers** — list active promo codes, often with expiry dates and terms.
- **Deals publishers** — surface sales, bundles, and limited-time offers without a code.

## On Involve Asia

Coupon/deals publishers are one of the most common publisher types on the platform, alongside [cashback publishers](cashback-publisher) and [deal aggregators](deal-aggregator). Advertisers can set specific terms for coupon use, since expired or unauthorised codes can affect validation.
