## What are clicks?

Clicks are the total number of times users click a [tracking link](tracking-link), sending them from a publisher's content toward an advertiser's [landing page](landing-page). It is the entry point of the [conversion flow](conversion-flow), recorded before anything about the purchase is known.

## Why clicks alone are not enough

- **No guarantee of earnings** — a click only earns [commission](commission) if it goes on to become a validated conversion.
- **Quality matters more than quantity** — a smaller number of well-matched clicks often converts better than a large number of untargeted ones.
- **Used with other metrics** — clicks are usually read alongside [impressions](impression), [click-through rate (CTR)](click-through-rate-ctr), and [conversion rate (CVR)](conversion-rate-cvr) rather than on their own.

## On Involve Asia

Click counts appear on every publisher's dashboard per offer and per [sub-ID](sub-id-sub-tracking), letting them see exactly which content or channel is generating traffic before it turns into earnings.
