## What is an affiliate ID?

An affiliate ID is the unique identifier assigned to a [publisher](publisher)'s account on an [affiliate network](affiliate-network). It is embedded in every [tracking link](tracking-link) the publisher generates, so the system knows exactly who should be credited for a click or sale.

## Where it is used

- **Link generation** — every deeplink or tracking link a publisher creates carries their affiliate ID.
- **Attribution** — conversions are matched back to the correct publisher account using this ID.
- **Reporting** — dashboards and payout records are all keyed to the affiliate ID.

## How it differs from a sub ID

The affiliate ID identifies the publisher account itself, while a [sub ID](sub-id-sub-tracking) is an extra tag a publisher adds to break their own traffic down further, for example by page, channel, or creative. Both work together to give a full picture of where conversions come from.
