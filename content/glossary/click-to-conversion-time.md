## What is click to conversion time?

Click to conversion time measures the gap between when a user clicks a [tracking link](tracking-link) and when they complete the action that counts as a [conversion](conversion-flow), such as a purchase. Some conversions happen within minutes; others take days.

## Why it varies

- **Purchase size** — a low-cost impulse buy tends to convert quickly, while a big-ticket item may involve days of comparison first.
- **Content type** — a review or comparison article often sends more considered traffic, which converts more slowly than a direct discount promotion.
- **Offer type** — sign-ups and leads can convert almost instantly, while multi-step purchases take longer.

## Why it matters

This figure needs to stay within the offer's [attribution window](attribution-window) and [cookie period](cookie-period) to count. Publishers promoting offers with a naturally long click to conversion time should favour offers with a longer cookie period, so late conversions still get credited.
