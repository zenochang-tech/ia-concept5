## What is commission capping?

Commission capping is a rule that sets an upper limit on the [commission](commission) an [advertiser](advertiser) will pay out on an offer. It protects the advertiser's margin on unusually large orders or high-volume periods, even when the standard [commission rate](commission-rate) would otherwise pay more.

## How it works

- **Per-order cap** — commission on any single order stops increasing past a set amount, regardless of order value.
- **Per-publisher cap** — a limit on the total commission one publisher can earn from the offer.
- **Time-based cap** — a ceiling on total commission the advertiser pays across a day, week, or campaign.

The specific rule applied is described by the offer's [capping type](capping-type).

## Why it matters

Publishers promoting high-value products should always check whether commission capping applies, since it can significantly reduce earnings compared with an uncapped [base payout](base-payout) on a large sale.
