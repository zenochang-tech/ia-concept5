## What is capping type?

Capping type is the rule that defines how a [commission capping](commission-capping) limit is applied on an offer. Rather than just saying a cap exists, it specifies where and how often that cap is enforced.

## Common capping types

- **Per order** — a maximum commission amount on any single sale, regardless of order value.
- **Per publisher** — a maximum total commission one publisher can earn from an offer, often within a set period.
- **Per day or per period** — a limit on total commission the advertiser will pay out across all publishers within a timeframe.

## Why it matters

Capping type affects how much a publisher can actually earn from an otherwise attractive [commission rate](commission-rate) or [base payout](base-payout). Publishers promoting high-ticket items should check whether a per-order cap applies, since it can significantly reduce commission on large sales.
