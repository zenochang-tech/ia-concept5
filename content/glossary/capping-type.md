## What is capping type?

Capping type is the rule that defines how a [commission capping](commission-capping) limit is applied on an offer, specifying whether it operates per order, per publisher, or per campaign period. Commission capping sets the ceiling itself; capping type describes the dimension that ceiling is measured against.

## The common capping types

- **Per order** — a maximum commission applies to any single sale, regardless of order value, with no carryover between transactions.
- **Per publisher** — a maximum total commission one publisher can earn from the offer over a set timeframe; once it is reached, further conversions stop earning.
- **Per campaign period** — the advertiser sets a total commission budget for the whole campaign; once it is used up across all publishers, payouts may slow or stop.

## Why it matters

Capping type affects how much a publisher can actually earn from an attractive [commission rate](commission-rate) or [base payout](base-payout). Publishers promoting high-ticket items or driving high volumes should check both the cap amount and its capping type before relying on the advertised rate.
