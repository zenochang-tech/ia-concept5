## What is mobile deep linking?

Mobile deep linking is the practice of building a [deeplink](deeplink) that opens a specific in-app screen, such as a product page or promo, rather than dropping the user on the app's generic home screen.

## Why it matters for publishers

- **Fewer drop-offs** — the user lands exactly where the offer is, without hunting through the app.
- **Works across states** — a well-built deep link can route a user correctly whether the app is already installed or needs installing first, tying into [app install tracking](app-install-tracking).
- **Higher conversion** — shorter paths to purchase generally convert better than generic app-store links.
