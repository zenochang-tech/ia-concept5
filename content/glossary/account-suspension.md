## What is account suspension?

Account suspension is the temporary or permanent removal of a [publisher](publisher)'s or advertiser's access to a platform account. It is typically triggered by a breach of the [affiliate program](affiliate-program) terms, suspected [affiliate fraud](affiliate-fraud), or a failure to meet [compliance](compliance) requirements.

## Common causes

- **Fraudulent activity** — fake clicks, [cookie stuffing](cookie-stuffing), or other invalid traffic practices.
- **Prohibited promotion methods** — using channels or tactics an advertiser has explicitly disallowed.
- **Unpaid or disputed obligations** — chargebacks, refund disputes, or unresolved compliance flags.
- **Inactivity or vetting failure** — accounts that never pass [publisher vetting](publisher-vetting) or go dormant for extended periods.

## What happens next

A suspended account usually loses the ability to generate new tracking links or receive new conversions. Pending earnings may be held during an investigation and are forfeited if the suspension is confirmed as fraud-related. Publishers and advertisers can typically appeal a suspension by providing evidence that their traffic or activity was legitimate.
