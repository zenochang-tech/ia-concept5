## What is last-click attribution?

Last-click attribution is an [attribution type](attribution-type) that credits a conversion to whichever publisher's link was clicked last before the customer completed the qualifying action, even if the customer clicked other publishers' links earlier in their journey.

## How it plays out

- **Multiple publishers involved** — if a customer clicks publisher A's link, browses, then clicks publisher B's link before buying, publisher B gets the credit.
- **[Attribution window](attribution-window)** — the last click only counts if it falls within the offer's tracking window before the purchase.
- **[Direct order](direct-order) vs [indirect order](indirect-order)** — last-click attribution usually decides which of these applies to a given sale.

## Why it matters

Last-click attribution is the most common model because it is simple to apply, but it can undervalue publishers who influence a customer earlier in the journey, such as through awareness content, even though they never got the final click.
