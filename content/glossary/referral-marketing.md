## What is referral marketing?

Referral marketing is a strategy where existing customers, users, or partners are encouraged to recommend a brand to people they know, in exchange for an incentive. It relies on personal trust: a recommendation from someone the buyer already knows tends to convert better than a cold advertisement.

## How it works with affiliate marketing

- **Structured through a [referral program](referral-program)** — with defined rewards and tracking.
- **Overlaps with [partner marketing](partner-marketing)** — referral schemes often run as one type of partnership within a wider [affiliate program](affiliate-program).
- **Rewarded like any other action** — the referrer typically earns a [commission](commission) or credit once the referred customer completes a qualifying action.

## Why it matters

Referral marketing extends an advertiser's reach through people who already use and like the product, making it a lower-cost, higher-trust complement to other performance marketing channels.
