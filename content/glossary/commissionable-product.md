## What is a commissionable product?

A commissionable product is an item in an [advertiser](advertiser)'s [product catalogue](product-catalog) that is eligible to earn [commission](commission) when a customer buys it through a tracked link. Not every product in a catalogue is necessarily commissionable.

## Why exclusions exist

- **Category exclusions** — some categories, such as gift cards or already-discounted items, are often excluded.
- **Different rates** — commissionable products can carry different [commission rates](commission-rate) depending on category or margin.
- **Listed on the offer** — exclusions and category rates are usually detailed on the [offer page](offer-page).

## Why it matters

Publishers who understand which products are commissionable can focus promotion on items that will actually convert into paid commission, rather than assuming every sale qualifies.
