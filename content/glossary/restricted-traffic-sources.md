## What are restricted traffic sources?

Restricted traffic sources are the traffic channels an [advertiser](advertiser) does not permit for a given [offer](affiliate-offer), even if other [promotion methods](promotion-method) are allowed. They narrow down where clicks are allowed to come from, separately from what content or technique is used to generate them.

## How this differs from prohibited promotion methods

- **Restricted traffic sources** — focus on the channel itself, such as a specific ad network, pop-up traffic, or incentivised traffic.
- **[Prohibited promotion methods](prohibited-promotion-methods)** — focus on the technique or behaviour, such as brand bidding or misleading claims.

Both are usually listed together in an offer's [allowed traffic sources](allowed-traffic-sources) and terms.

## Why it matters

Sending traffic from a restricted source can result in commissions being voided for [compliance](compliance) reasons, regardless of how the offer was promoted. Publishers should check an offer's traffic source rules before launching any new channel.
