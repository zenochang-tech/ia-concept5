## What is publisher vetting?

Publisher vetting is the process an [advertiser](advertiser) uses to review a [publisher](publisher) before letting them promote an [offer](affiliate-offer). It typically looks at the publisher's [properties](property), the traffic they send, and the content or channels they plan to use.

## What advertisers check

- **Property quality** — whether the website, app, or social account looks legitimate and active.
- **Traffic sources** — whether the publisher's promotion methods match the offer's allowed traffic sources.
- **Track record** — past performance, compliance history, and any flags on the account.

## How it fits the application flow

Vetting usually happens after a publisher applies to an offer, and it determines the offer's [application status](application-status). Some offers use [manual approval](manual-approval) so an advertiser can vet each applicant individually, while others approve automatically once basic criteria are met.
