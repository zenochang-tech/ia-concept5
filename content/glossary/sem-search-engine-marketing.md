## What is SEM?

SEM (Search Engine Marketing) is a [promotion method](promotion-method) where a [publisher](publisher) pays to place ads on search engine results pages, usually by bidding on keywords relevant to an [offer](affiliate-offer). It is a form of [paid search traffic](paid-search-traffic), distinct from unpaid, organic ranking.

## How it is used in affiliate marketing

- **Bidding on [recommended keywords](recommended-keyword)** — terms an advertiser suggests are likely to convert.
- **Avoiding [negative keywords](negative-keyword)** — terms an advertiser has explicitly ruled out.
- **Respecting [brand bidding](brand-bidding) rules** — many advertisers restrict or ban bidding on their own brand name.

## Why it matters

SEM can drive fast, high-intent traffic, but because it is paid and highly visible, advertisers tend to set strict rules for it. Publishers should always confirm an offer allows SEM, and check its keyword rules, before running paid search campaigns, since this is distinct from unpaid [SEO](seo-search-engine-optimization).
