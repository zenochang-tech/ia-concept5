## What is cross-device tracking?

Cross-device tracking follows a user across more than one device during their shopping journey, for example clicking a [publisher's](publisher) link on mobile, then finishing the purchase later on a laptop.

## Why it's difficult

Standard tracking relies on a cookie stored on the device where the click happened. When a customer switches devices, that cookie isn't visible to the second device, which can cause a genuine sale to go untracked.

- **Cookie-based tracking** — works within one browser or device, but breaks across devices.
- **Login-based matching** — some platforms link activity to a signed-in account instead, closing part of the gap.

## Why it matters

Without some form of cross-device tracking, publishers can lose credit for real sales they influenced, since correct [attribution](attribution-window) depends on recognising the same user later on a different device. It remains one of the harder problems in affiliate tracking compared with straightforward [last-click attribution](last-click-attribution).
