## What is a referral program?

A referral program is the structured version of [referral marketing](referral-marketing): a defined scheme with clear rules, tracking, and rewards for anyone who refers a new customer that completes a qualifying action. It turns word-of-mouth into something measurable and repeatable.

## Typical structure

- **Referral link or code** — given to each referrer to track who sent the new customer.
- **Qualifying action** — the referred customer must do something specific, such as sign up or make a purchase.
- **Reward** — usually a [commission](commission), credit, or fixed bonus paid to the referrer.

## On Involve Asia

A referral program can run as its own campaign or sit within a wider [affiliate program](affiliate-program), with [publishers](publisher) tracked and paid through the same infrastructure used for other offers.
