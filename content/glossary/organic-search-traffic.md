## What is organic search traffic?

Organic search traffic refers to visitors who click through to a publisher's page from unpaid, natural listings on a search engine, rather than from a paid advertisement. It's driven by content ranking well on its own merit.

## How it's built

- **[SEO](seo-search-engine-optimization)** — optimising content, structure, and keywords so pages rank higher without paying for placement.
- **Content quality** — in-depth reviews, comparisons, and guides tend to earn and hold organic rankings better than thin pages.

## Why advertisers care

- **Traffic source rules** — many offers explicitly allow organic search traffic while restricting or requiring approval for [paid search traffic](paid-search-traffic).
- **Sustainability** — organic traffic tends to be lower cost and longer-lasting for [content publishers](content-publisher), since it doesn't depend on ongoing ad spend.

Publishers should check an offer's allowed traffic sources, since some advertisers treat organic and paid search very differently.
