## What is compliance?

Compliance is whether a [publisher](publisher)'s promotion of an offer sticks to the rules set by the [advertiser](advertiser), the [terms & conditions](terms-conditions-t-cs) of the program, and relevant advertising law.

## What it typically covers

- **Promotion methods** — using only [promotion methods](promotion-method) the advertiser allows, and avoiding [prohibited promotion methods](prohibited-promotion-methods).
- **Disclosure** — clearly labelling affiliate content, often via an [affiliate disclosure statement](affiliate-disclosure-statement).
- **Brand use** — following rules on trademarks, brand bidding, and how the advertiser's name or assets are used.

## Why it matters

Non-compliance can lead to a commission being void, an offer being paused for a publisher, or in serious cases an account suspension. Staying compliant protects both a publisher's earnings and the advertiser's brand.
