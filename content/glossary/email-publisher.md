## What is an email publisher?

An email publisher is a [publisher](publisher) whose main channel is [email marketing](email-marketing): sending offers, deals, or tracked links to their own subscriber list rather than publishing on a website or app.

## What sets this type apart

- **Owned audience** — the subscriber list belongs to the publisher, unlike traffic from search or social.
- **Direct delivery** — offers reach subscribers without depending on a platform's algorithm.
- **Stricter rules** — many advertisers set specific [promotion method](promotion-method) terms for email, such as required disclosures or restrictions on subject lines and sending frequency.

## Why it matters

Because email lists are a direct line to an audience, advertisers often ask email publishers to follow clear rules before approving them, covering things like list consent and how the brand is represented in a campaign.
