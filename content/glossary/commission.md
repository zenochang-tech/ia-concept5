## What is a commission?

A commission is the reward a [publisher](publisher) earns for driving a result for an [advertiser](advertiser). It is the core of the performance model: the advertiser only pays when a sale, lead, or other qualifying action actually happens.

## How commission is calculated

Advertisers set their own rates. Two things shape the payout:

- **The commission model** — such as cost per sale (CPS), cost per lead (CPL), or cost per install (CPI).
- **The rate** — a percentage of the sale value (for example, 4.2% of every order) or a flat fee per action.

## Validation and cookie period

Two more factors decide whether, and when, a commission is paid:

- **Validation period** — the advertiser confirms the sale is genuine and not cancelled before it pays out.
- **Cookie period** — how long after a click a purchase still counts for the publisher.

Both are shown on each offer, so publishers know the terms before they promote.
