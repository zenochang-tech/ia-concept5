## What is an existing customer offer?

An existing customer offer is a promotion or [commission rate](commission-rate) that an advertiser sets specifically for shoppers who have already bought from them before, rather than for new customers.

## Why advertisers separate the two

- **New customer offers** — often pay a higher commission or feature a stronger discount, since acquiring a first-time buyer is more valuable to the advertiser.
- **Existing customer offers** — usually pay a lower rate, or exclude commission entirely, since the advertiser is retaining rather than acquiring.

## Why it matters

Publishers promoting an offer should check its [validation terms](validation-term) and [offer page](offer-page) carefully, since a sale to an existing customer may earn a different, often lower, commission than the same sale from someone buying for the first time.
