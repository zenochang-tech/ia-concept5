## What is cost per unique click (CPUC)?

Cost per unique click (CPUC) is a payment model where a [publisher](publisher) is paid a fixed amount for each unique visitor who clicks their link, rather than for every click that visitor makes.

## How it differs from raw clicks

A single visitor can click a link several times in one session, refreshing the page or clicking twice by mistake. CPUC filters this out:

- **Unique click** — counted once per visitor within a set window, however many times they click.
- **Raw click** — every click is counted, which can inflate volume without reflecting real traffic.

## Why it matters

CPUC gives advertisers a cleaner measure of genuine interest and protects publisher payouts from being diluted by duplicate clicks. It sits alongside other pricing models such as [cost per action (CPA)](cost-per-action-cpa), and is usually reviewed next to [click-through rate (CTR)](click-through-rate-ctr) when assessing campaign performance.
