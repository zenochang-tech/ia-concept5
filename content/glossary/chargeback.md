## What is a chargeback?

A chargeback is the reversal of a sale that had already passed [validation](validation) and been credited to a [publisher](publisher). It typically happens after a refund, a cancelled order, a returned product, or a payment dispute.

## Why chargebacks happen

- **Returns and refunds** — the customer sends the product back or gets their money refunded.
- **Cancelled orders** — the order never actually ships or completes.
- **Fraud or disputes** — the payment itself is later found to be invalid.

## Effect on commission

When a chargeback occurs, the commission tied to that transaction is removed from the publisher's earnings, even if it was previously marked as validated. This is why earnings figures can look final and later adjust; it is a normal part of how [compliance](compliance) with advertiser payment terms is maintained, not a sign of an error in tracking.
