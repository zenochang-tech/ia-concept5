## What is a hybrid commission model?

A hybrid commission model combines two or more [commission models](commission-model) within a single offer, rather than paying on just one type of action. For example, an offer might pay a fixed fee for every completed lead, plus an additional percentage if that lead goes on to make a purchase.

## Common combinations

- **CPL + CPS** — a fixed [cost per lead](cost-per-lead-cpl) payout, plus a percentage-based [cost per sale](cost-per-sale-cps) if the lead converts further.
- **Flat fee + percentage** — a base amount for the action itself, plus a share of the transaction value.
- **Tiered add-ons** — a hybrid model can also layer in a [tiered commission structure](tiered-commission-structure), so higher volumes unlock a better rate on top of the base terms.

## Why it matters

Hybrid models let advertisers reward the full customer journey rather than a single step, and they give publishers more than one way to earn from the same piece of traffic, under one overall [commission structure](commission-structure).
