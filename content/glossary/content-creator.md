## What is a content creator?

A content creator is a [publisher](publisher) who earns [commission](commission) by producing original content, such as reviews, tutorials, or videos, that recommends products and links to [advertisers](advertiser).

## Where they operate

- **Blogs and articles** — long-form reviews and buying guides.
- **Video and social** — YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram content featuring products.
- **Newsletters** — curated recommendations sent directly to subscribers.

## Relation to other publisher types

Content creators overlap with [influencers](influencer) and [KOLs](kol-key-opinion-leader), though the term generally emphasises the content itself over personal following or reach. Their promotion often qualifies as [sponsored content](sponsored-content) and should be disclosed as such.
