## What is sponsored content?

Sponsored content is a blog post, video, social media post, or other piece of content that a [content creator](content-creator) or [influencer](influencer) produces and publishes on behalf of an advertiser, usually for payment, a free product, or commission. It differs from organic content because the advertiser has paid for, or otherwise compensated, its creation.

## Common forms

- **Blog reviews and roundups** — a dedicated post covering a product, service, or brand.
- **Social media posts** — a paid post, story, or video on Instagram, TikTok, or similar platforms.
- **Video content** — a dedicated segment or full video built around the advertiser's product.
- **Newsletter mentions** — a featured section in an email publisher's regular send.

## Disclosure

Because sponsored content is a paid arrangement, most markets require it to be clearly labelled (for example, "sponsored" or "ad"). Publishers running sponsored content alongside an [affiliate program](affiliate-program) typically combine both: a flat fee or product for the content itself, plus commission on any sales it drives through their tracking link. Following the advertiser's [promotion method](promotion-method) rules keeps the content compliant and the commission valid.
