## What are terms & conditions (T&Cs)?

Terms and conditions (T&Cs) are the specific rules an [advertiser](advertiser) sets for their [affiliate program](affiliate-program) or an individual offer. They spell out what a [publisher](publisher) can and cannot do when promoting it, and what has to be true for a tracked action to earn [commission](commission).

## What T&Cs typically cover

- **Allowed and prohibited [promotion methods](promotion-method)** — for example, whether paid search or brand-name bidding is permitted.
- **Eligible countries** — which regions or audiences the offer can be promoted to.
- **Cookie and [validation](validation) period** — how long a click counts, and how long the advertiser has to confirm a sale.
- **Exclusions** — order types, customer segments, or products that don't earn commission.

## Why it matters

Commission is only paid when an action complies with the offer's T&Cs. A publisher who ignores a restriction, such as using a prohibited channel, risks having conversions rejected or their account suspended, even if the traffic and sales look genuine. Reading the T&Cs before promoting an offer is standard practice for any publisher.
