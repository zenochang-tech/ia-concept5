## What is an affiliate disclosure statement?

An affiliate disclosure statement is a notice a [publisher](publisher) places on their content to tell readers that some links are [affiliate links](affiliate-link), and that a purchase through them may earn the publisher a commission. It is a transparency requirement, not a marketing line.

## Where and how it appears

- **Placement** — usually near the top of an article, video description, or post, before the reader reaches the links.
- **Wording** — plain language stating the relationship, for example noting that the publisher may be compensated for purchases made through the links.
- **Consistency** — applied across all monetised content, not just individual posts.

## Why it matters

Regulators in many markets require this kind of disclosure, and advertisers expect their publishers to follow it as part of general [compliance](compliance). Publishers who skip disclosure risk complaints, platform penalties, or removal from an [affiliate program](affiliate-program).
