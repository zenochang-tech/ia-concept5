## What is a negative keyword?

A negative keyword is a term or phrase that an advertiser adds to a [PPC publisher](ppc-publisher)'s exclusion list, stopping ads from showing whenever someone searches for it. It is the opposite of a keyword the publisher is bidding on.

## Why advertisers use them

- **Protecting the brand name** — the most common use is preventing publishers from bidding on the brand's own name, a form of [brand bidding](brand-bidding).
- **Controlling budget and quality** — excluding irrelevant or low-intent searches (for example, "free", "jobs", or a competitor's name) keeps [paid search traffic](paid-search-traffic) relevant.

## On Involve Asia

Negative keyword lists are usually published as part of an offer's promotion rules. Publishers running search campaigns are expected to check and follow them, since bidding on an excluded term can lead to a rejected commission or account action.
