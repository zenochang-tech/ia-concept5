## What is a commission model?

A commission model is the method an [advertiser](advertiser) uses to work out how much a [publisher](publisher) earns for each qualifying action. It decides what event triggers a payout and how that payout is calculated.

## Common commission models

- **Cost per sale (CPS)** — a percentage of the order value.
- **Cost per lead (CPL)** — a fixed fee per qualifying lead, such as a form submission.
- **Cost per install (CPI)** — a fixed fee per app install.
- **Cost per action (CPA)** — a fixed fee per defined action, such as a sign-up.

## Where it's set

Each offer specifies its commission model alongside the [commission rate](commission-rate), so publishers know upfront how earnings are calculated before they promote it.
