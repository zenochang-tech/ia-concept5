## What is a property?

A property is a specific website, app, or social media channel that a [publisher](publisher) registers in order to promote offers. A publisher account can hold one or several properties, and each one describes where and how the publisher actually reaches their audience.

## What a property covers

- **Websites and blogs** — content or comparison sites.
- **Apps** — mobile applications, relevant to [app publishers](app-publisher).
- **Social accounts** — pages or profiles used for [promotion](promotion-method), such as an Instagram or YouTube channel.

## Why it matters

Advertisers often review a publisher's properties as part of [publisher vetting](publisher-vetting) before approving them for an offer, since the audience, traffic quality, and content on a property all affect whether it is a good fit for a given brand.
