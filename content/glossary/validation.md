## What is validation?

Validation is the review stage between a [conversion](conversion-creation) being recorded and its [commission](commission) being paid. A click that completes a purchase, sign-up, or install is captured immediately, but the commission isn't paid out at that point, it first enters a validation queue.

## What happens during validation

- **Order checks** — the advertiser confirms the order wasn't cancelled or returned.
- **Fraud and compliance checks** — the advertiser confirms the lead wasn't fraudulent and the promotion method used was approved.
- **Eligibility checks** — the advertiser confirms the action met all of the offer's qualifying conditions.

## Why it matters

Validation protects advertisers from paying commission on conversions that are later reversed, fraudulent, or non-compliant, for example through a [chargeback](chargeback). The length of this review is set by the offer's [validation term](validation-term), typically 14 to 60 days, which is why reported earnings often show a mix of pending and validated amounts.
