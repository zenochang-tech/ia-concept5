## What is validation?

Validation is the process by which an [advertiser](advertiser) reviews a tracked conversion to confirm it's genuine before [commission](commission) is finalised and paid to the [publisher](publisher). A sale might be tracked immediately but still be pending validation until the advertiser checks it against returns, cancellations, and fraud checks.

## What happens during validation

- **Order checks** — the advertiser confirms the order was completed and paid for.
- **Returns and cancellations** — any order returned or cancelled within the return window is rejected.
- **Fraud checks** — suspicious or duplicate orders may be flagged and excluded.

## Why it matters

Commission is only guaranteed once a conversion passes validation, which is why reported earnings often show a mix of pending and validated amounts. The length of this review is set by the offer's [validation term](validation-term), and rejected conversions (for example, due to a [chargeback](chargeback)) don't earn commission, even though they appeared in initial tracking.
