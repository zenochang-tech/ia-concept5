## What is a validation term?

A validation term is the length of time an [advertiser](advertiser) allows itself to review a tracked conversion before deciding whether it counts. It's the timeframe attached to the [validation](validation) process, for example, 30 or 60 days, and is shown on the offer page alongside details like the [commission rate](commission-rate).

## Why it exists

- **Returns and cancellations** — many products have a return window that needs to close before a sale can be confirmed as final.
- **Fraud review** — some checks, such as identifying duplicate or fraudulent orders, take time to complete.
- **Fair payout timing** — it protects the advertiser from paying commission on sales that are later reversed, such as through a [chargeback](chargeback).

## How it affects publishers

Until the validation term ends, a conversion typically shows as pending in the publisher's reporting rather than confirmed. Publishers promoting offers with longer validation terms should expect a longer wait between a sale happening and the commission being finalised, separate from the [cookie period](cookie-period), which only governs how long a click stays eligible for attribution.
