## What is a validation term?

A validation term is the window of time an [advertiser](advertiser) needs to assess a tracked conversion before approving or rejecting the commission. During this period, the advertiser checks whether the order was completed, whether it falls within the offer's [commissionable products](commissionable-product) and [commissionable countries](commissionable-country), and whether the sale has since been cancelled, returned, or flagged as fraudulent.

## Why it exists

Validation terms protect advertisers from paying commission on transactions that don't represent genuine revenue, for example an order that's returned within the return window, or reversed through a [chargeback](chargeback). Holding the commission until the term passes ensures only confirmed sales are paid.

## How long it takes

Validation terms vary significantly across advertisers and industries. A fast-moving consumer goods advertiser may validate within days, while a travel or financial services offer may take weeks or months, reflecting longer fulfilment cycles or more complex eligibility checks. Each offer lists its validation term on its offer page, so publishers can gauge how long [validation](validation) typically takes before applying.
