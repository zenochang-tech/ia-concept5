## What is SEO?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is a [promotion method](promotion-method) where a [publisher](publisher) optimises their content and website so it ranks well in unpaid search results. It builds [organic search traffic](organic-search-traffic), as opposed to the paid placements bought through [SEM](sem-search-engine-marketing).

## How it is used in affiliate marketing

- **Content publishers** write reviews, guides, and comparisons designed to rank for relevant searches, a common approach for [content publishers](content-publisher).
- **On-page optimisation** covers titles, headings, and structure that help search engines understand and rank a page.
- **Sustained traffic** builds up over time, unlike paid ads that stop the moment spend stops.

## Why it matters

SEO tends to produce durable, ongoing traffic to an offer once content ranks well, making it a favoured strategy for publishers who invest in long-form content. Because it is organic, it also falls outside most of the strict traffic-source rules that apply to paid channels.
