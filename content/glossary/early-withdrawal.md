## What is early withdrawal?

Early withdrawal is an Involve Asia payout option that shortens the time between a [commission](commission) being approved and the [publisher](publisher) actually receiving it. It applies to earnings that have already passed [validation](validation) and are sitting in "pending advertiser collection", waiting on the advertiser to transfer funds to Involve Asia, so the publisher does not have to wait out that gap.

## How it differs from other withdrawal types

- **Standard withdrawal** — pays out once earnings are validated and the advertiser's funds have been collected, following the usual payment cycle.
- **Early withdrawal** — releases already-approved commissions sooner, before the advertiser's transfer completes; it does not apply to conversions still being validated.
- **Express withdrawal** — a related but earlier-stage feature that can release funds while a conversion is still in "pending validation"; see [express withdrawal](express-withdrawal).

## Eligibility and processing

- Only available on offers where the advertiser has enabled it, not across every program.
- Requests are typically processed within 5 working days, excluding weekends and Malaysian public holidays.
- The publisher's eligible balance must still meet the [minimum withdrawal amount](minimum-withdrawal-amount).
- Requested from the Payment section of the publisher dashboard.
