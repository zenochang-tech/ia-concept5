## What is early withdrawal?

Early withdrawal is an option that lets a [publisher](publisher) request payout of earnings that have already passed [validation](validation), ahead of the advertiser's normal payment cycle.

## How it differs from a standard withdrawal

- **Standard withdrawal** — follows the advertiser's set payout schedule, for example monthly or net-30.
- **Early withdrawal** — releases eligible funds sooner, sometimes with a fee or a cap on how much can be withdrawn early.
- **Express withdrawal** — a related but distinct feature focused on speed of processing once a withdrawal is requested; see [express withdrawal](express-withdrawal).

## Why it matters

Early withdrawal helps publishers manage cash flow without waiting out a full payment cycle, provided their [current earnings](current-earnings) meet the [minimum withdrawal amount](minimum-withdrawal-amount) and any other terms the advertiser sets.
