## What is deduplication?

Deduplication is the process of checking tracked conversions for duplicates, such as the same order appearing twice, so only one is counted and paid as [commission](commission).

## When duplicates happen

- **Multiple clicks, one sale** — a customer clicks several publishers' links before buying; deduplication decides which one gets credit, usually the last one via [last-click attribution](last-click-attribution).
- **Technical retries** — a page reload or a tracking pixel firing twice can create a duplicate record of the same order.
- **Matching by click ID** — systems compare the [click ID](click-id) attached to each conversion to spot repeats.

## Why it matters

Without deduplication, an advertiser could be charged twice for one sale, and multiple publishers could wrongly claim commission for the same order. It's applied during [validation](validation), before a conversion is confirmed and paid.
