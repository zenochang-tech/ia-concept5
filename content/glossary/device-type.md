## What is device type?

Device type is a data point attached to a [click](clicks) or conversion showing what kind of hardware the visitor used, typically desktop, mobile, or tablet.

## Why it's tracked

- **Performance comparison** — publishers can see whether their traffic converts better on mobile or desktop.
- **App vs browser distinction** — for app-based offers, device type helps separate app traffic, relevant to [app install tracking](app-install-tracking).
- **Troubleshooting** — helps explain gaps when a sale started on one device and finished on another, a case covered by [cross-device tracking](cross-device-tracking).

## On Involve Asia

Device type appears in reporting alongside other click attributes, letting publishers and advertisers break down performance by platform rather than only looking at totals. It is especially useful for offers that rely on [mobile deep linking](mobile-deep-linking).
