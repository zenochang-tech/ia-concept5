## What is link cloaking?

Link cloaking is the practice of hiding a long [tracking link](tracking-link) behind a shorter, cleaner URL, typically one that uses the publisher's own domain instead of the raw affiliate link with all its tracking parameters.

## Why publishers cloak links

- **Readability** — a short branded link is easier to type, remember, and share than a long string of parameters.
- **Trust** — some users are wary of clicking an obviously long [affiliate link](affiliate-link) or [deeplink](deeplink); a cloaked link looks cleaner.
- **Organisation** — cloaked links are often paired with a clear [link title](link-title), making it easier for the publisher to track which link is which.

## Why it matters

Cloaking changes how a link looks, not how it tracks. As long as the underlying tracking parameters are preserved, a cloaked link still attributes clicks and conversions to the correct publisher.
