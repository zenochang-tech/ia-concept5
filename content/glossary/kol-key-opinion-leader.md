## What is a KOL (Key Opinion Leader)?

A KOL, short for Key Opinion Leader, is a [publisher](publisher) with an established audience and a reputation that gives their recommendations real weight. The term is common across Southeast Asia and is largely used interchangeably with [influencer](influencer).

## How KOLs promote offers

- **Product endorsements** — reviewing or featuring a product across [social media](social-media) posts or videos.
- **Sponsored content** — paid or commission-based [sponsored content](sponsored-content) that blends promotion with the KOL's usual content style.
- **Trackable links or codes** — a personal tracking link or promo code so any resulting sale is credited to them.

## KOL vs influencer

Both describe a publisher whose audience trusts their opinion. "KOL" is more common in markets such as Malaysia, Indonesia, and China, while "influencer" is the more globally used term. On Involve Asia, both fall under the same [content creator](content-creator) publisher category.
