## What is a click ID?

A click ID is a unique identifier created the moment someone clicks a [tracking link](tracking-link). It travels with the click, often as a parameter in the URL, and is later used to connect a conversion back to that specific click.

## Why it exists

- **Accurate attribution** — without a click ID, there would be no reliable way to know which click led to which sale.
- **Deduplication** — click IDs help detect and prevent the same conversion being counted twice, supporting [deduplication](deduplication).
- **Troubleshooting** — when a publisher raises a [missing conversions](missing-conversions) query, the click ID is usually the first thing checked.

## On Involve Asia

Every tracked click gets its own click ID automatically. It works alongside a [sub-ID](sub-id-sub-tracking), which a publisher can add themselves, to give both the platform and the publisher a precise record of where each conversion came from.
