## What is the Involve App?

The Involve App is Involve Asia's mobile application, built so [publishers](publisher) can check their performance and manage their account without needing to log into the desktop [affiliate dashboard](affiliate-dashboard).

## What publishers can do in the app

- **Track earnings** — view [current earnings](current-earnings) and performance in close to [real time](real-time-commission-tracking).
- **Manage links** — generate or look up tracking links and deeplinks while on the move.
- **Withdraw funds** — request a payout, including through [Express Withdrawal](express-withdrawal), directly from the app.

## Why it matters

Many publishers, especially influencers and content creators who work primarily from a phone, use the Involve App as their main way to check performance between posts, rather than switching to a desktop dashboard.
