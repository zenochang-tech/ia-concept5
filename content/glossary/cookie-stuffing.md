## What is cookie stuffing?

Cookie stuffing is a form of [affiliate fraud](affiliate-fraud) where a [tracking cookie](tracking-cookie) is placed on a visitor's device without them actually clicking a genuine [affiliate link](affiliate-link), so any later purchase is wrongly credited as a conversion.

## How it's done

- **Hidden iframes or pixels** — loading tracking code invisibly on unrelated pages.
- **Forced redirects** — briefly routing a visitor through a tracking link without their knowledge.
- **Bulk cookie drops** — stuffing many cookies at once to capture unrelated future purchases.

## Why it's taken seriously

Cookie stuffing is closely related to [click fraud](click-fraud) and [click-jacking](click-jacking). It is against most advertiser and platform policies, and detected accounts risk having commission voided or facing account suspension.
