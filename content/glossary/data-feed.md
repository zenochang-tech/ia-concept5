## What is a data feed?

A data feed is a structured file, usually CSV or XML, that lists an advertiser's products or offers with details such as price, image, category, and a trackable link. [Publishers](publisher) import it to build listings without adding items one by one.

## What it's used for

- **Product catalogues** — comparison sites and marketplaces use feeds to display up-to-date pricing and stock.
- **Deal and coupon sites** — feeds can highlight discounted items automatically.
- **Bulk deeplinking** — feeds often include ready-made tracked links instead of requiring a separate [deeplink generator](deeplink-generator) step for every product.

## On Involve Asia

Where an advertiser provides a data feed, publishers can access it from the [offer page](offer-page) and refresh it periodically, since prices, stock, and availability in a [product catalogue](product-catalog) change often.
