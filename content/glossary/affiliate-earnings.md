## What are affiliate earnings?

Affiliate earnings are the total [commission](commission) a [publisher](publisher) has accumulated across all the offers and advertisers they work with. The figure covers every validated action, not just a single campaign or offer.

## How earnings are broken down

- **Estimated earnings** — commission from actions that have not yet cleared the advertiser's [validation](validation) period.
- **Current earnings** — commission that has been validated and is confirmed as owed.
- **Paid earnings** — commission already withdrawn or transferred to the publisher.

## Why it matters

Tracking affiliate earnings this way lets a publisher see not just what they have made, but what is still in progress. It also helps them plan withdrawals and judge which offers or advertisers are actually worth their promotional effort.
