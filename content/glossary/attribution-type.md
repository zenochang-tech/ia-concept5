## What is attribution type?

Attribution type is the rule an [advertiser](advertiser) uses to decide which [publisher](publisher) gets credit for a conversion, particularly when a customer clicked more than one affiliate link before buying. It sets the logic behind every [conversion](conversion-creation) recorded on the platform.

## Common attribution types

- **Last-click attribution** — credit goes to the publisher whose link was clicked most recently before the purchase.
- **First-click attribution** — credit goes to the publisher whose link the customer clicked first in their journey.
- **Position-based or custom rules** — less common models that split credit across multiple touchpoints.

## Why it matters

The attribution type an advertiser chooses directly affects which publishers get paid, especially in categories where customers compare offers across several sites before buying. Most platforms, including Involve Asia, default to [last-click attribution](last-click-attribution) within the offer's [attribution window](attribution-window) unless stated otherwise.
