## What is click origin country?

Click origin country is the country recorded for a specific click on a [tracking link](tracking-link), based on the visitor's IP address at that moment. Unlike [audience country](audience-country), which describes a publisher's overall audience, this is measured click by click.

## How it is used

- **Geo-Lock** — Involve Asia enforces offer [geo-targeting](geo-targeting) using Geo-Lock: a click from outside an offer's allowed [offer region](offer-region) can be shown an error page instead of the merchant site, blocking the conversion.
- **Publisher checks** — publishers can review an offer's tracking and geo-lock details before promoting it, so they know which countries are covered.
- **Reporting** — platforms often break down clicks by origin country so publishers can see where their real traffic is coming from.

## Why it matters

A publisher's audience may generally sit in one country but still generate clicks from elsewhere, for example through shared links or VPN use. Click origin country gives advertisers a precise, per-click basis for applying geo-targeting and commission rules, rather than relying on audience country alone.
