## What is click origin country?

Click origin country is the country recorded for a specific click on a [tracking link](tracking-link), based on the visitor's location at that moment. Unlike [audience country](audience-country), which describes a publisher's overall audience, this is measured click by click.

## How it is used

- **Geo-targeting** — offers restricted to certain markets can be enforced using click origin country, part of an offer's [geo-targeting](geo-targeting) rules.
- **Commissionable country** — a click from outside the offer's [commissionable country](commissionable-country) list may still be tracked but will not earn commission.
- **Reporting** — platforms often break down traffic by click origin country so publishers can see where their real clicks are coming from.

## Why it matters

A publisher whose audience is generally based in one country may still see clicks from elsewhere, for example through shared links or VPN use. Click origin country gives advertisers a more precise, per-click basis for applying targeting and commission rules than audience country alone.
