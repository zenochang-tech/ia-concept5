## What is affiliate fraud?

Affiliate fraud is any deceptive tactic a [publisher](publisher) uses to generate [commission](commission) that was not honestly earned. It undermines the trust that [advertisers](advertiser) place in the performance model, where payment is only meant to follow a genuine action.

## Common forms

- **Click fraud** — generating fake or automated clicks to inflate traffic numbers.
- **Cookie stuffing** — dropping a [tracking cookie](tracking-cookie) on a user without their knowledge or consent.
- **Incentivised or fake traffic** — paying users to click or convert purely to trigger a payout.
- **Self-referral** — a publisher buying through their own links to claim commission on their own purchases.

## How it is caught

Advertisers and networks review conversions during [validation](validation), checking for patterns like unusually short time-to-conversion, duplicate accounts, or traffic that never matches genuine buying behaviour. Confirmed fraud typically results in reversed commission and can lead to [account suspension](account-suspension).
