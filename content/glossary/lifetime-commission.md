## What is lifetime commission?

Lifetime commission is a [commission structure](commission-structure) where a publisher keeps earning from a customer they referred for as long as that customer remains active on the advertiser's platform, rather than being paid only for the first purchase.

## Lifetime vs other commission types

- **[One-time commission](one-time-commission)** — paid once, for the first qualifying action only.
- **[Recurring commission](recurring-commission)** — paid on a repeating basis, such as every subscription renewal, usually for a defined period.
- **Lifetime commission** — similar to recurring, but with no fixed end date; the publisher earns for as long as the referred customer stays active.

## Why it matters

Lifetime commission rewards publishers for the long-term value of the customers they bring in, not just the first transaction, which can make certain offers far more valuable over time than their headline rate suggests.
