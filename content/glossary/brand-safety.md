## What is brand safety?

Brand safety is the effort to make sure an [advertiser](advertiser)'s name and offers only appear alongside content that matches its reputation and values. In an [affiliate program](affiliate-program), that means checking who is allowed to promote a brand and how.

## How it is maintained

- **Publisher vetting** — reviewing a publisher's site, content, and audience before approving them for an offer, see [publisher vetting](publisher-vetting).
- **Promotion rules** — offers list [prohibited promotion methods](prohibited-promotion-methods), such as misleading claims or placement on adult or hateful content.
- **Ongoing monitoring** — advertisers and platforms can review where and how a link is being used after approval, not only at sign-up.

## Why it matters

A single bad placement, such as a link appearing next to offensive content or a misleading claim, can damage an advertiser's reputation regardless of how well the campaign performs. Brand safety works alongside [compliance](compliance) checks to keep an affiliate programme both effective and reputationally sound.
