## What is conversion rate (CVR)?

Conversion rate (CVR) measures how effectively [clicks](clicks) on a publisher's link turn into completed conversions. It is calculated as the number of conversions divided by the number of clicks, shown as a percentage.

## Why it varies

- **Traffic quality** — visitors genuinely interested in an offer convert at a higher rate than broad, untargeted traffic.
- **Offer fit** — a strong match between content and offer usually lifts CVR.
- **Landing experience** — where a click lands, and the [click to conversion time](click-to-conversion-time) it takes, affects whether the visitor completes the action.

## Why it matters

CVR is a key performance indicator alongside [click-through rate (CTR)](click-through-rate-ctr) and [earnings per click (EPC)](earnings-per-click-epc), helping publishers judge which offers are worth continued promotion.
