## What is a recommended keyword?

A recommended keyword is a search term an [advertiser](advertiser) suggests a [publisher](publisher) target when building [SEM](sem-search-engine-marketing) or [SEO](seo-search-engine-optimization) campaigns for their offer. It points publishers towards language that reliably reaches interested shoppers, such as a product category or a common search phrase.

## How it is used

- **Guiding paid search campaigns** — helping publishers choose terms likely to convert.
- **Guiding organic content** — informing article titles and on-page keywords for SEO.
- **Staying within brand terms** — recommended keywords are chosen to avoid clashing with rules on [brand bidding](brand-bidding).

## Why it matters

Recommended keywords work alongside [negative keywords](negative-keyword), which list terms a publisher must avoid. Together they give publishers clear guidance on what a keyword strategy for an offer should, and should not, include.
