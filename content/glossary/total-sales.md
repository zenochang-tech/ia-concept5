## What is total sales?

Total sales is the full monetary value of purchases attributed to a [publisher](publisher)'s tracked clicks over a given period, such as a day, week, or month. It reflects everything recorded before [validation](validation), so the figure can still change once cancelled or returned orders are removed.

## Where it's used

- **Reporting dashboards** — total sales is usually shown alongside clicks and conversions to give a quick view of performance.
- **Commission calculation** — the [commission rate](commission-rate) is applied against total sales (or the validated portion of it) to work out payout.
- **Comparing offers or periods** — publishers use total sales to see which offers, campaigns, or months performed best.

## Total sales vs validated sales

Total sales reflects everything tracked, while validated sales reflects only what the advertiser has confirmed as genuine after the [validation](validation) period. Related figures like [average order value](average-order-value-aov) and [GMV](gross-merchandise-value-gmv) help put the total sales number in context, showing not just how much was sold but the typical size of each order.
