## What is a price comparison publisher?

A price comparison publisher builds pages or tools that show a product's price across multiple merchants at once, so shoppers can pick the cheapest or best-value option before clicking through to buy.

## How they operate

- **Product-level focus** — unlike a general [comparison publisher](comparison-publisher), which may compare broader categories or brands, price comparison publishers usually work at the individual product level, pulling data from each merchant's [product catalogue](product-catalog).
- **High purchase intent** — shoppers arriving through a price comparison page are usually close to buying, since they've already decided on the product.

## Why merchants work with them

Price comparison publishers help merchants win price-sensitive sales without competing purely on ad spend. Since these publishers rely on accurate, up-to-date pricing, merchants need to keep listings current, or risk commission disputes over price mismatches.
