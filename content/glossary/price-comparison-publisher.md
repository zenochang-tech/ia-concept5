## What is a price comparison publisher?

A price comparison publisher runs a site or app that lists the same or similar products from multiple merchants side by side, so shoppers can spot the best price before clicking through to buy.

## How they operate

- **Multi-advertiser by design** — unlike a publisher promoting a single brand, a price comparison publisher works with many advertisers at once within a product category, pulling listings from each merchant's [product catalogue](product-catalog).
- **Data-driven, not manual** — pages rely on structured, frequently updated product data (price, availability, retailer links) rather than manual write-ups or reviews.

## On Involve Asia

Each product comes with a pre-built [affiliate link](affiliate-link) that already carries tracking, so publishers can show several retailers for the same product without building links by hand.
