## What is a deeplink?

A deeplink is a [tracking link](tracking-link) that sends a visitor straight to a specific page, product, or category on an advertiser's site or app, instead of the generic homepage.

## Why publishers use it

Sending someone directly to the relevant product removes an extra step and reduces the chance they leave before finding what brought them there.

- **Web deeplink** — points to an exact URL within the merchant's website.
- **App deeplink** — opens a specific in-app screen, used with [mobile deep linking](mobile-deep-linking).

## Creating a deeplink

Not every offer or URL supports this; whether one does is marked as [deeplinkable](deeplinkable). Where it's supported, publishers typically build the link through a [deeplink generator](deeplink-generator) rather than editing tracking parameters by hand.
