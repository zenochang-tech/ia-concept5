## What is Instant Apply?

Instant Apply is an offer setting that approves a [publisher's](publisher) application automatically, so they can start promoting an [affiliate program](affiliate-program) right away instead of waiting for a review.

## Instant Apply vs manual approval

- **Instant Apply** — the application is approved the moment it is submitted, and the publisher's [application status](application-status) updates to approved instantly.
- **[Manual approval](manual-approval)** — the advertiser reviews each application before deciding whether to approve it, which can take longer but allows more control over who joins.

## Why it matters

Instant Apply speeds up [onboarding](onboarding) for publishers, letting them generate tracking links and start driving traffic without delay. Advertisers tend to use it for offers with fewer compliance concerns, reserving manual approval for programmes that need closer vetting of who promotes them.
