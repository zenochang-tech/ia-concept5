## What are estimated earnings?

Estimated earnings is a projected [commission](commission) figure shown to a [publisher](publisher) before the underlying conversions have completed [validation](validation), giving an early sense of likely payout.

## Estimated vs current or confirmed earnings

- **Estimated earnings** — a forecast based on tracked activity that hasn't been fully checked yet.
- **Current earnings** — the running total once conversions have started moving through validation; see [current earnings](current-earnings).
- **Confirmed earnings** — the final, validated amount an advertiser has agreed to pay.

## Why it matters

Because a validation period can result in some conversions being reduced or rejected (for example, a cancelled order), estimated earnings can shift before it becomes final [affiliate earnings](affiliate-earnings). Publishers should treat it as a guide rather than a guaranteed amount.
