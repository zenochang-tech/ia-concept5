## What is manual approval?

Manual approval is an offer setting where the [advertiser](advertiser) reviews each [publisher's](publisher) application before deciding whether to accept them into the [affiliate program](affiliate-program), rather than approving every applicant automatically.

## How it works

- **Application review** — the advertiser checks a publisher's site, audience, or promotional methods before approving.
- **[Application status](application-status)** — while under review, the application shows as pending until the advertiser makes a decision.
- **[Publisher vetting](publisher-vetting)** — manual approval is often part of a broader vetting process, especially for offers with strict brand or compliance requirements.

## Manual approval vs Instant Apply

Manual approval trades speed for control. Advertisers use it when they want to check fit before granting access, while offers with fewer concerns often use [Instant Apply](instant-apply) instead, approving applications the moment they are submitted.
