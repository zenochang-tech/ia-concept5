## What is offer currency?

Offer currency is the currency in which an advertiser defines the terms of an offer, such as the commission rate or a flat payout per action. It usually matches the currency used in the [offer region](offer-region) where the merchant sells.

## Why it's shown separately

- **Multi-market platforms** — a publisher promoting offers across several countries will see different offer currencies side by side.
- **Not the same as payout** — the offer currency is set by the advertiser, but a publisher's actual earnings may be converted into their own [payout currency](payout-currency).
- **Reporting clarity** — dashboards often let publishers switch between offer currency and a single [reporting currency](reporting-currency) for consistency across campaigns.

## On Involve Asia

Each offer page states its currency clearly, so publishers can compare rates accurately before promoting.
