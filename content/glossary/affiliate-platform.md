## What is an affiliate platform?

An affiliate platform is the software that powers an [affiliate program](affiliate-program), giving [advertisers](advertiser) and [publishers](publisher) the tools to work together. Involve Asia is one such platform, serving markets across Southeast Asia.

## Core functions

- **Link and deeplink generation** — creating trackable URLs for any offer or product.
- **Conversion tracking** — recording clicks and matching them to sales, leads, or installs.
- **Reporting** — dashboards showing performance for both sides of the relationship.
- **Payouts** — calculating and distributing [commission](commission) once conversions are validated.

## Why it matters

Without a shared platform, advertisers and publishers would need to track sales and pay commission manually, which does not scale. A platform automates the whole loop, from the first click to the final payout, and gives both sides visibility into what is working.
