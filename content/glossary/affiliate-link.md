## What is an affiliate link?

An affiliate link is the unique URL a [publisher](publisher) uses to promote an advertiser's [affiliate offer](affiliate-offer). It works like any other link, but carries the publisher's [affiliate ID](affiliate-id) so the network can credit them for any resulting click or sale.

## How it works

- **Generation** — a publisher creates the link from the [affiliate dashboard](affiliate-dashboard) for an offer they are approved to promote.
- **Redirection** — clicking it routes the user through the network's tracking domain before landing on the advertiser's page.
- **Attribution** — a [tracking cookie](tracking-cookie) or click ID records the visit, so a purchase within the [cookie period](cookie-period) is matched back to the publisher.

## Affiliate link vs deeplink

A standard affiliate link usually points to an advertiser's homepage or a general landing page. A [deeplink](deeplink) does the same job but points to a specific product or page deep within the advertiser's site, while still preserving tracking.
