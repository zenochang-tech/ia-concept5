## What is reporting currency?

Reporting currency is the currency in which a publisher's performance figures, such as clicks, conversions, and estimated earnings, are shown in the [affiliate dashboard](affiliate-dashboard). It is a display setting, separate from the currency an offer is priced in or the currency a publisher is actually paid in.

## How it differs from other currencies

- **Reporting currency** — used only to display figures consistently in the dashboard.
- **[Offer currency](offer-currency)** — the currency the advertiser sets prices and rates in.
- **[Payout currency](payout-currency)** — the currency a publisher actually receives on withdrawal.

## Why it matters

Publishers working across markets often deal with offers priced in several currencies. A consistent reporting currency lets them compare performance across offers at a glance, even though the underlying commission may have been earned in a different currency.
