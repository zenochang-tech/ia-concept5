## What is a commissionable country?

A commissionable country is a country that an [advertiser](advertiser) has approved for payouts on a given offer. If a conversion happens outside this list, it will not earn [commission](commission), even if the click and sale are otherwise valid.

## How it's used

- **Set per offer** — each offer defines its own list of commissionable countries, alongside its [offer region](offer-region).
- **Checked at validation** — the customer's location, or [click origin country](click-origin-country), is what matters, not just the publisher's location.
- **Linked to region lock** — offers with a [region lock](region-lock) restrict commission strictly to these countries.

## Why it matters

Publishers should check an offer's commissionable countries, and its intended [audience country](audience-country), before promoting it to markets the advertiser has not approved for payout.
