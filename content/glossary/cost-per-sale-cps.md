## What is cost per sale (CPS)?

Cost per sale (CPS) is the most common [commission model](commission-model) in affiliate marketing, where a [publisher](publisher) earns a percentage of the order value for each validated sale their link generates.

## How it works

- **Percentage-based** — the [commission rate](commission-rate) is a share of the sale value, for example 5% of the order total.
- **Validated before payout** — the sale must clear the advertiser's [validation](validation) checks, such as not being returned or cancelled.
- **Tracked per order** — commission is usually calculated on the full order, though some offers exclude certain commissionable products.

## How it compares

Unlike [cost per lead (CPL)](cost-per-lead-cpl) or [cost per action (CPA)](cost-per-action-cpa), which pay a flat fee, CPS scales with order value, making it common for retail and e-commerce offers.
