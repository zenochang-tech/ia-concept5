## What is an offer page?

An offer page is where a publisher finds everything they need to evaluate and promote a specific offer: its [offer name](offer-name), commission structure, [offer region](offer-region), and [offer currency](offer-currency).

## What it typically includes

- **Commission terms** — the rate or flat fee, plus the cookie period and validation term.
- **Promotion rules** — allowed and restricted traffic sources, and any negative keywords.
- **Tracking tools** — the [tracking link](tracking-link) or deeplink a publisher generates to start driving traffic.

## Why it matters

Before applying to promote an offer, publishers rely on the offer page to check whether it fits their audience and platform. On Involve Asia, this is also where publishers pull the exact link they'll share, so every click is correctly attributed back to them.
