## What is a commission structure?

A commission structure is the complete set of rules an [advertiser](advertiser) uses to decide how a [publisher](publisher) is paid on an offer. It combines the [commission model](commission-model), the [commission rate](commission-rate), and any additional conditions.

## What it can include

- **Flat structure** — one rate applies to every qualifying action.
- **Tiered structure** — the rate increases as a publisher hits higher sales volumes, known as a [tiered commission structure](tiered-commission-structure).
- **Capped structure** — a maximum payout limit, or [commission capping](commission-capping), applies per action or period.
- **Hybrid structure** — a mix of models, such as a base fee plus a percentage.

## Why it matters

A clear commission structure lets publishers judge whether an offer is worth promoting and estimate earnings accurately before committing traffic to it.
