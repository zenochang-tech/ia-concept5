## What is performance marketing?

Performance marketing is an approach where advertisers only pay when a specific, measurable outcome happens, such as a sale, lead, or app install, rather than paying for exposure regardless of results.

## How it differs from traditional advertising

- **Pay for outcomes, not exposure** — spend is tied directly to [cost per action](cost-per-action-cpa), so budget scales with actual results.
- **Lower risk for advertisers** — there's no cost if the campaign doesn't convert, unlike upfront media buys.
- **Trackable by design** — every action needs to be attributed correctly, which is why tracking links and cookies matter so much in this model.

## Where affiliate marketing fits

Affiliate marketing, the model Involve Asia runs on, is a form of performance marketing: publishers earn a [commission](commission) only once a tracked action is validated, making it a specific, relationship-based branch of the wider [partner marketing](partner-marketing) space.
