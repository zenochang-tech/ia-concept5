## What are UTM parameters?

UTM parameters are short tags appended to the end of a URL (for example, `?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email`) that record details such as traffic source, medium, and campaign name. Analytics tools like Google Analytics read these tags to report where traffic came from.

## Common UTM parameters

- **utm_source** — where the traffic originated, such as a specific newsletter or site.
- **utm_medium** — the general channel, such as email, social, or paid search.
- **utm_campaign** — the name of the specific campaign or promotion.

## UTM parameters vs affiliate tracking

UTM parameters sit alongside, but separately from, an affiliate platform's own tracking. A publisher's [tracking link](tracking-link) already attributes sales and commission through its own identifiers, while UTM parameters are mainly for the publisher's or advertiser's own analytics. A publisher can add UTM tags to a tracking link for their own reporting without affecting how the platform tracks the click, similar in purpose to a [Sub-ID](sub-id-sub-tracking) but read by a different system.
