## What is social media as a promotion method?

Social media, as a [promotion method](promotion-method), refers to publishers reaching their audience through platforms such as Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, or YouTube, rather than a website or email list. It is the main channel used by [influencers](influencer), [KOLs](kol-key-opinion-leader), and [content creators](content-creator).

## How it is used

- **Posts and stories** — sharing product recommendations, reviews, or discount codes with followers.
- **Short-form video** — a fast-growing format for showcasing products and offers.
- **Bio links and swipe-ups** — directing followers to a tracked link or code.

## Why it matters

Social media promotion tends to rely more on personal trust and audience relationship than search-based methods, which is why advertisers often review a publisher's following and content style during [publisher vetting](publisher-vetting) before approving them for a social-focused campaign.
