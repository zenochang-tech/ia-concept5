## What is a link title?

A link title is a custom name a publisher gives a [tracking link](tracking-link) or [deeplink](deeplink) at the point it is created, so it is easy to recognise later without reading the full URL.

## How it's used

- **Organisation** — labelling links by placement, such as "homepage banner" or "Instagram bio", makes reporting easier to scan.
- **Performance tracking** — combined with a [sub-ID](sub-id-sub-tracking), a link title helps a publisher see which specific placement or campaign is performing best.
- **Reference later** — publishers can look up a past link by its title in their [deeplink history](deeplink-history) instead of searching by URL.

## Why it matters

As a publisher creates more links across different pages, placements, and campaigns, clear link titles are what keep reporting readable instead of a list of near-identical URLs.
